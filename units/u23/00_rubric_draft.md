# Unit 23 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 **Year B** — Unit 23
**Topic:** **Length & Time** — measuring length with metres and kilometres; calculating the perimeter of
squares, rectangles and triangles; connecting decimal notation to the metric system and converting between
metres and kilometres; comparing perimeter and area; calculating elapsed time (bridging, rounding); reading
24-hour time and timetables; and solving duration problems in 12- and 24-hour time.
**Source:** `DoE Unit 23.zip` → `Mathematics-Stage3-Unit23.docx`, 8 lessons.
**Year note:** **Year B** unit (the "Year A" strings are prior-learning cross-references).
**Strands / outcomes (from the SC tables + the "Outcomes and content" grid, Table 30):**
- `MA3-GM-02` — *selects and uses the appropriate unit and device to measure lengths and distances including perimeters* — content groups **A** (measure, perimeter) and **B** (decimal notation, metric conversion, length-comparison problems)
- `MA3-NSM-02` — *measures and compares duration, using 12- and 24-hour time* — content groups **A** (24-hour time, timetables, duration) and **B** (elapsed time, bridging, rounding, duration)
- `MA3-2DS-02` — *area of rectangles* — content group **A** (perimeter/area comparison)
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **Banding — cognitive demand (per `feedback_solo_band_a_heavy_units`, set by Nick 2026-06-20).** Unit 23 is
> an early Year B unit (gradual release) with an A/B mix. Banded by **SOLO cognitive demand**:
> recall/single-step/foundational → **Red**; applying/multi-step/reasoning → **Yellow** — across both A and B
> content. The A/B group label is recorded on every content point. Green = Stage 4 **Core**.

## 0a. Flat pool of extracted success criteria ("Students can …")

1. explain the equivalence of whole-number and decimal representations of length; interpret and record lengths/distances using decimal notation *(GM-B; L1, L2)*
2. use decimal place value to convert between metres and kilometres *(GM-B; L1, L2)*
3. convert measurements to the same unit to compare lengths/distances; relate size of unit to number of units *(GM-B; L2, L4)*
4. calculate perimeters of common 2D shapes (squares, rectangles, triangles) *(GM-A; L3)*
5. use efficient strategies to calculate the perimeter of a large rectangular area in metres *(GM-A; L4)*
6. determine which side lengths are needed to find the perimeter of a shape *(GM-A; L4)*
7. recognise that rectangles with the same perimeter may have different dimensions *(GM-A; L5)*
8. investigate and compare perimeters of rectangles with the same area *(GM-B; L5)*
9. calculate the area of rectangles using familiar metric units *(2DS-A; L5)*
10. use start and finish times to calculate the elapsed time of events *(NSM-B; L6)*
11. add and subtract time mentally using bridging strategies; round answers to the nearest minute/hour *(NSM-B; L6)*
12. read time using appropriate 24-hour time language; compare and convert 12- and 24-hour time *(NSM-A; L7)*
13. read, interpret and use timetables from real-life situations (12- and 24-hour time) *(NSM-A; L7)*
14. solve a variety of problems involving duration, including 12-hour and 24-hour notation *(NSM-A/B; L7, L8)*
15. use a variety of measuring devices to measure lengths/distances; use metres and kilometres *(GM-A; L8)*

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

### 🔴 RED — recall / single-step / foundational

**R1 — Measure length with metres and kilometres**
*"I can use metres and kilometres and the right measuring device to measure lengths and distances."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(rulers, tape measures, trundle wheel)*
Covers SC 15. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-02`). Content points: "Use a variety of measuring devices to measure lengths and distances in different contexts"; "Use metres and kilometres for length and distances."*

**R2 — Calculate the perimeter of squares, rectangles and triangles**
*"I can calculate the perimeter of a square, rectangle or triangle by adding the side lengths."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 4. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-02`). Content point: "Calculate perimeters of common two-dimensional shapes, including squares, rectangles and triangles."*

**R3 — Record and interpret lengths in decimal notation**
*"I can record and interpret lengths in decimal notation, and explain that 1500 m and 1.5 km are the same."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 1. *Geometric measure **B** (`MA3-GM-02`). Content points: "Recognise the equivalence of whole-number and decimal representations of measurements of length"; "Interpret decimal notation for lengths and distances"; "Record lengths and distances using decimal notation."*

**R4 — Read and convert 12- and 24-hour time**
*"I can read 24-hour time and convert between 12-hour and 24-hour time."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 12. *Non-spatial measure **A** (`MA3-NSM-02`). Content points: "Compare 12- and 24-hour time systems and convert between them"; "Read time using appropriate 24-hour time language."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying / multi-step / reasoning

**Y1 — Convert between metric units of length**
*"I can convert between metres and kilometres using place value, and convert to the same unit to compare lengths."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 2, 3. *Geometric measure **B** (`MA3-GM-02`). Content points: "Use decimal place value system to convert between metres and kilometres"; "Convert measurements to the same unit to compare lengths and distances"; "Explain and use the relationship between the size of a unit and the number of units needed."*

**Y2 — Find the side lengths needed and calculate perimeter**
*"I can work out which side lengths I need, and use efficient strategies to find the perimeter of large rectangles."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 5, 6. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-02`). Content points: "Use efficient strategies to calculate the perimeter of a large rectangular area in metres"; "Determine which side lengths are needed to find the perimeter of a shape."*

**Y3 — Compare perimeter and area of rectangles**
*"I can show that rectangles with the same perimeter can have different dimensions, and compare the perimeters of rectangles with the same area."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(grid paper)*
Covers SC 7, 8, 9. *Geometric measure **A**/**B** (`MA3-GM-02`) & Two-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-2DS-02`). Content points: "Recognise that rectangles with the same perimeter may have different dimensions"; "Investigate and compare perimeters of rectangles with the same area"; "Calculate the area of rectangles using familiar metric units."*

**Y4 — Calculate elapsed time**
*"I can calculate elapsed time from start and finish times using bridging, and round to the nearest minute or hour."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 10, 11. *Non-spatial measure **B** (`MA3-NSM-02`). Content points: "Use start and finish times to calculate the elapsed time of events"; "Add and subtract time mentally using bridging strategies"; "Round answers to time calculations to the nearest minute or hour."*

**Y5 — Read timetables and solve duration problems**
*"I can read and use 12- and 24-hour timetables and solve duration problems with them."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 13, 14. *Non-spatial measure **A**/**B** (`MA3-NSM-02`). Content points: "Read, interpret and use timetables from real-life situations, involving 12- and 24-hour time"; "Solve a variety of problems involving duration, including where times are expressed in 12-hour and 24-hour notation."*

Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y4, Y5. *Stage 4 — **Ratios and rates** (`MA4-RAT-C-01`). Content points: "Interpret distance–time graphs"; "Describe a journey represented on a distance–time graph."*
