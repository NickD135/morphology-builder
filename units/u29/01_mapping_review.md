## 🔴 Red

### R1 — Compare the properties of prisms and pyramids
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-01` (Three-dimensional spatial structure **A**) — *3D objects: Compare, describe and name prisms and pyramids*:
  > "Compare properties of prisms and pyramids"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L1** (Properties of prisms and pyramids) — sorts/compares faces, edges, vertices of prisms vs pyramids; reinforced in L2 and L3.

### R2 — Name prisms and pyramids by the shape of their base
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-01` (3D-spatial **A**) — *3D objects: Compare, describe and name prisms and pyramids*:
  > "Name prisms and pyramids according to the shape of their base"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L1** (introduces the naming convention); reinforced **L3**.

### R3 — Interpret decimal notation for thousandths
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-02` (Represents numbers **A**) — *Decimals and percentages: Recognise that the place value system can be extended beyond hundredths*:
  > "Express thousandths as decimals"
  > "Interpret decimal notation for thousandths"
  > "Indicate the place value of digits in decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L6** (Connecting decimals to measurements of capacity) — the unit's one explicit decimal-place-value lesson (Table 32: thousandths ticked in column 6).

### R4 — Recognise equivalent whole-number and decimal capacity measurements
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-02` (3D-spatial **A**) — *Volume: Connect decimal representations to the metric system*:
  > "Recognise the equivalence of whole-number and decimal representations of measurements of capacities"
  > "Interpret decimal notation for capacities"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L5** (Equivalent capacities), reinforced **L6** and **L7**.

## 🟡 Yellow

### Y1 — Decide if a diagram is a net, and name the solid it folds into
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-01` (3D-spatial **A**) — *3D objects: Connect three-dimensional objects with two-dimensional representations*:
  > "Examine a diagram to determine whether it is or is not the net of a closed 3-dimensional object"
  > "Visualise and name prisms and pyramids, given representations of their nets (Reasons about spatial visualisation)"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L2** (Visualise and name nets).

### Y2 — Sketch 3D objects from different views
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-01` (3D-spatial **A**) — *3D objects: Connect three-dimensional objects with two-dimensional representations*:
  > "Visualise and sketch three-dimensional objects from different views, including top, front and side views (Reasons about spatial orientation)"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L3** (Sketching different views of three-dimensional objects).

### Y3 — Construct prisms and pyramids (skeletal models and from views)
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-01` (Three-dimensional spatial structure **B**) — *3D objects: Construct prisms and pyramids*:
  > "Create skeletal models of prisms and pyramids"
  > "Construct three-dimensional models of prisms and pyramids, given drawings of different views"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L4** (Visualising and constructing models). *(The unit's only group-B content.)*

### Y4 — Select and use appropriate units to measure capacity
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-02` (3D-spatial **A**) — *Volume: Choose appropriate units of measurement for capacity*:
  > "Select and use appropriate units to measure the capacities of a variety of containers"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L6** and **L7** (Measuring and recording capacities) — Table 32 ticks columns 6 & 7.

### Y5 — Record capacity measurements to 3 decimal places
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-02` (3D-spatial **A**) — *Volume: Connect decimal representations to the metric system*:
  > "Record measurements to 3 decimal places"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L5, L6, L7, L8** (recording runs through the whole capacity/volume sequence — Table 32 ticks columns 5–8); home lesson **L7** (Measuring and recording capacities to 3 decimal places).

### Y6 — Use displacement to investigate and compare volumes
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-02` (3D-spatial **A**) — *Volume: Use displacement to investigate volumes of irregular solids*:
  > "Recognise that an object's volume takes up space by observing the change in water level when an object is placed in a container of water"
  > "Compare the volumes of 2 or more objects by marking the change in water level when each is submerged in a container"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L8** (Using displacement to investigate volume).

## 🟢 Green — Stage 4 (no DoE Unit 29 lesson — expected)

All three Green outcomes map to **Stage 4 Volume `MA4-VOL-C-01`** (syllabus p.62).
There is **no DoE Unit 29 lesson** for any of them — they are original extension
mini-lessons. (Stage 1 records this explicitly rather than inventing a DoE source.)

### G1 — Find the volume of a prism using V = base area × height
- **NESA:** `MA4-VOL-C-01` (Stage 4 Volume) — *Develop and apply the formula to find the volume of a prism to solve problems*:
  > "Develop the formula for the volume of a prism: V = base area×height, leading to V = Ah"
  > "Apply the formula for the volume of a prism to prisms with uniform cross-sections to solve problems"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original mini-lesson). Builds on Stage 3 B layer-counting, which is outside this DoE unit.

### G2 — Convert between metric units of volume and capacity
- **NESA:** `MA4-VOL-C-01` (Stage 4 Volume) — *Choose appropriate units of measurement for volume and capacity and convert between units*:
  > "Choose an appropriate unit to measure the volume or capacity of different objects and justify the choice"
  > "Convert between metric units of volume and capacity (1 cm3 = 1000 mm3, 1 cm3 = 1 mL, 1 m3 = 1000 L = 1 kL, 1000 kL = 1 ML)"
  > "Solve practical problems involving the volume and capacity of right prisms and cylinders"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original mini-lesson). Extends R3/R4/Y4.

### G3 — Represent prisms from different 2D views and identify their uniform cross-section
- **NESA:** `MA4-VOL-C-01` (Stage 4 Volume) — *Describe the different views of prisms and solids that have been formed from prism combinations*:
  > "Represent prisms from different views in 2 dimensions, including top, side, front and back views"
  > "Identify and illustrate the cross-sections of different prisms"
  > "Examine the idea that prisms have a uniform cross-section that is equal to the base area"
  > "Determine if a particular solid has a uniform cross-section"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original mini-lesson). Extends Y2.
