### ⚠️ Band-rule note for Unit 29 (read before the gate)

Spec §0b's validated rule is **content group A → Red (Year 5 consolidation), group
B → Yellow (Year 6 home content)** — and §0b explicitly says to *"confirm it per unit
against the DoE unit's own framing… a reliable starting point, not a trap."*

Unit 28 fit that rule cleanly because it deliberately paired the **A and B** content
groups of each sub-strand (Position A+B, Chance A+B). **Unit 29 is structurally
different.** Its content is almost entirely content group **A** (3D-spatial A + Volume/
capacity A + Decimals A), with **only one** group-B sub-strand present — *3D objects:
Construct prisms and pyramids* (`MA3-3DS-01` **B**). A strict A→Red / B→Yellow split
would produce **~10 Red, 1 Yellow, 3 Green** — no usable SOLO progression for students.

Therefore Unit 29 is banded by **SOLO cognitive demand within the Stage 3 content**,
with the syllabus content-group letter shown against every outcome for transparency:

- **Red** = unistructural / multistructural **recall & identification** (name & compare
  prisms; interpret decimal place value; recognise capacity equivalence).
- **Yellow** = relational **applying & spatial reasoning** — the Year-6 "do it / reason
  it" work (visualise nets, sketch & construct 3D objects, select units, record to 3 dp,
  investigate volume by displacement). This includes the genuine group-**B** construct
  content (Y3).
- **Green** = Stage 4 (`MA4-VOL-C-01`).

This is the one Stage-0 judgement call for Nick to confirm or override at review.

### 🔴 RED — recall & identification (Stage 3, mostly group A consolidation)

**R1 — Compare the properties of prisms and pyramids**
*"I can compare prisms and pyramids by their faces, edges and vertices, and describe what makes a prism a prism and a pyramid a pyramid."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(handling geometric models / everyday packages makes faces–edges–vertices concrete)*
Covers SC 1. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-3DS-01`). 3D objects: Compare, describe and name prisms and pyramids — content point: "Compare properties of prisms and pyramids."*

**R2 — Name prisms and pyramids by the shape of their base**
*"I can name a prism or pyramid from the shape of its base (e.g. triangular prism, hexagonal pyramid)."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 2. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-3DS-01`). content point: "Name prisms and pyramids according to the shape of their base."*

**R3 — Interpret decimal notation for thousandths**
*"I can read and interpret decimals to 3 decimal places, naming the place value of each digit, knowing the place-value system extends past hundredths to thousandths."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 8. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-02`). Decimals and percentages: Recognise that the place value system can be extended beyond hundredths — content points: "Express thousandths as decimals"; "Interpret decimal notation for thousandths"; "Indicate the place value of digits in decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places."*

**R4 — Recognise equivalent whole-number and decimal capacity measurements**
*"I can recognise when two capacity measurements are equal across whole-number and decimal forms (e.g. 1500 mL = 1.5 L = 1.500 L) and interpret decimal notation for capacities."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(pouring equal capacities from a measuring jug into a shared container makes equivalence concrete)*
Covers SC 9, 10. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-3DS-02`). Volume: Connect decimal representations to the metric system — content points: "Recognise the equivalence of whole-number and decimal representations of measurements of capacities"; "Interpret decimal notation for capacities."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying & spatial reasoning (Stage 3 Year-6 home work)

**Y1 — Decide if a diagram is a net, and name the solid it folds into**
*"I can examine a diagram and decide whether it is the net of a closed 3D object, and visualise and name the prism or pyramid a given net folds up to make."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(cutting and folding nets, matching nets to models — the spatial-visualisation concept is unreliable to teach on the page alone)*
Covers SC 3, 4. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-3DS-01`). 3D objects: Connect three-dimensional objects with two-dimensional representations — content points: "Examine a diagram to determine whether it is or is not the net of a closed 3-dimensional object"; "Visualise and name prisms and pyramids, given representations of their nets (Reasons about spatial visualisation)."*

**Y2 — Sketch 3D objects from different views**
*"I can visualise a 3D object and sketch its top, front and side views, and recognise an object from drawings of its different views."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(sketching from a held model, then checking by turning it)*
Covers SC 5. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-3DS-01`). content point: "Visualise and sketch three-dimensional objects from different views, including top, front and side views (Reasons about spatial orientation)."*

**Y3 — Construct prisms and pyramids (skeletal models and from views)**
*"I can build skeletal models of prisms and pyramids, and construct a 3D model of a prism or pyramid from drawings of its different views."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(straws/toothpicks + connectors for skeletons; the only genuine group-B content in the unit)*
Covers SC 6, 7. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-3DS-01`). 3D objects: Construct prisms and pyramids — content points: "Create skeletal models of prisms and pyramids"; "Construct three-dimensional models of prisms and pyramids, given drawings of different views."*

**Y4 — Select and use appropriate units to measure capacity**
*"I can choose a sensible unit (mL or L) and an appropriate measuring tool to measure the capacity of a container, and use it to measure accurately."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(measuring real containers with measuring cylinders / jugs)*
Covers SC 11. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-3DS-02`). Volume: Choose appropriate units of measurement for capacity — content point: "Select and use appropriate units to measure the capacities of a variety of containers."*

**Y5 — Record capacity measurements to 3 decimal places**
*"I can read a measuring scale and record a capacity to 3 decimal places (e.g. 1.275 L), using decimal place value correctly."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(reading graduated scales on real measuring equipment)*
Covers SC 12. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-3DS-02`). Volume: Connect decimal representations to the metric system — content point: "Record measurements to 3 decimal places."*

**Y6 — Use displacement to investigate and compare volumes**
*"I can show that an object takes up space by watching the water level rise when it is submerged, and compare the volumes of two or more objects by marking the change in water level."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(displacement: water, containers, submerged objects — Archimedes)*
Covers SC 13, 14. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-3DS-02`). Volume: Use displacement to investigate volumes of irregular solids — content points: "Recognise that an object's volume takes up space by observing the change in water level when an object is placed in a container of water"; "Compare the volumes of 2 or more objects by marking the change in water level when each is submerged in a container."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 group A (verified against NESA K-10 syllabus)

No DoE Unit 29 lesson teaches these — expected for Green.

All Green outcomes use **Stage 4 group A** content from the **Volume** focus area
(`MA4-VOL-C-01` — *applies knowledge of volume and capacity to solve problems involving
right prisms and cylinders*). This is the genuine Stage-4 generalisation beyond the unit:
where Stage 3 B *counts* layers of cubic-centimetre blocks, Stage 4 establishes the
**formula** `V = Ah` and formal unit conversion.

> ⚠️ Deliberately **not** used for Green: **surface area of right prisms** is **Stage 5**
> (`MA5-ARE-C-01` — *"solves problems involving the surface area of right prisms"*), past
> the Stage 4 A pitch, so it is excluded. **Calculating volumes of rectangular prisms by
> counting cm³ layers** is **Stage 3 group B** (`MA3-3DS-01` B Volume), not Stage 4 — so
> it is *not* used as a Green outcome either.

**G1 — Find the volume of a prism using V = base area × height**
*"I can find the volume of a prism by multiplying the area of its base by its height (V = Ah), and apply this to rectangular and triangular prisms with a uniform cross-section."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y6 / the Stage 3 B layer-counting. *Stage 4 — **Volume** (`MA4-VOL-C-01`). Content points: "Develop the formula for the volume of a prism: V = base area×height, leading to V = Ah"; "Apply the formula for the volume of a prism to prisms with uniform cross-sections to solve problems."*

**G2 — Convert between metric units of volume and capacity**
*"I can choose an appropriate unit for a volume or capacity and convert between metric units (e.g. 1 cm³ = 1 mL, 1 m³ = 1000 L = 1 kL), and use this to solve practical problems."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R3/R4/Y4. *Stage 4 — **Volume** (`MA4-VOL-C-01`). Content points: "Choose an appropriate unit to measure the volume or capacity of different objects and justify the choice"; "Convert between metric units of volume and capacity (1 cm3 = 1000 mm3, 1 cm3 = 1 mL, 1 m3 = 1000 L = 1 kL, 1000 kL = 1 ML)"; "Solve practical problems involving the volume and capacity of right prisms and cylinders."*

**G3 — Represent prisms from different 2D views and identify their uniform cross-section**
*"I can represent a prism from its top, side, front and back views in 2D, identify and illustrate the cross-section of a prism, and recognise that a prism has a uniform cross-section equal to its base area."*
Band: **Green** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(slicing/illustrating cross-sections; pairs with Y2's sketch-views work)*
Extends Y2. *Stage 4 — **Volume** (`MA4-VOL-C-01`). Content points: "Represent prisms from different views in 2 dimensions, including top, side, front and back views"; "Identify and illustrate the cross-sections of different prisms"; "Examine the idea that prisms have a uniform cross-section that is equal to the base area"; "Determine if a particular solid has a uniform cross-section."*
