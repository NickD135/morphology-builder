# Unit 37 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 Year B — Unit 37
**Topic:** **Multiplicative relations & measurement** — products, factors and primes; the area model for
multiplication; area of rectangles (metric units) and the same-perimeter/different-dimensions relationship;
area of parallelograms and triangles by dissection & rearrangement; the multiplicative (layer) structure of
volume; volume of rectangular prisms in cubic centimetres and cubic metres; efficient multi-digit
multiplication; and volume problem solving.
**Source:** `DoE Unit 37.zip` → `Mathematics-Stage3-Unit37.docx`, 8 lessons (Overview / Beginning / Core /
Concluding template; SC live inside the per-lesson **"Syllabus — Learning intentions and success criteria"**
tables, which also carry the DoE sub-strand label and A/B group). Number-sense activities 5–8 (percentages,
10% off, 10% sales tax — resources 21–23) are **warm-up number sense, not in the unit's "Outcomes and
content" grid**, so they are not formal SOLO outcomes here; surfaced in Beyond/number-sense context only.
**Strands / outcomes (from the SC tables + the "Outcomes and content" grid, Table 24):**
- `MA3-MR-01` — *selects and applies strategies to solve problems involving multiplication and division with whole numbers* (Lessons 1, 8) — content groups **A** (products/factors/area model) and **B** (efficient 4-digit strategies)
- `MA3-GM-02` — *Geometric measure: length / perimeter* (Lesson 2) — content group **A**
- `MA3-2DS-02` — *Two-dimensional spatial structure: area* (Lessons 2, 3, 4) — content group **A** (rectangle area) and **B** (parallelogram/triangle area)
- `MA3-2DS-03` — *Two-dimensional spatial structure: 2D shapes — transformations/dissection* (Lessons 3, 4) — content group **B**
- `MA3-3DS-02` — *Three-dimensional spatial structure: volume* (Lessons 5, 6, 7, 8) — content group **B**
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **A/B read from the DoE "Outcomes and content" grid (Table 24), not from difficulty.** The grid puts
> **Multiplicative relations A** (product term, factors, prime/composite, area model), **Geometric measure A**
> (same-perimeter rectangles) and **Two-dimensional spatial structure A** (rectangle area in metric units)
> in **group A**; and **Multiplicative relations B** (efficient 4-digit multiplication), **Two-dimensional
> spatial structure B** (dissect/rearrange shapes; parallelogram & triangle area) and **Three-dimensional
> spatial structure B** (all volume) in **group B**.
>
> **Banding (policy set 2026-06-20).** Every **group A** content point goes in **Red** (mandatory) — so R1–R5
> are all group A. Group B content is then banded by **SOLO cognitive demand**: the foundational, single-step
> volume work (the layer structure of volume and calculating volume in cm³) sits in **Red** even though it is
> group B (the rule explicitly allows simple B content in Red); the applying/multi-step group-B work
> (parallelogram & triangle area via dissection, volume in m³, efficient 4-digit multiplication, volume word
> problems) sits in **Yellow**. Green = Stage 4 **Core** content.

6. compare the areas of rectangles that have the same perimeter *(2DS-A; L2)*
7. show how rectangles with the same perimeter can have different dimensions *(GM-A / 2DS-A; L2)*

10. calculate the area of a parallelogram *(2DS-B; L3)*
11. recognise that a triangle has half the area of a parallelogram with the same base and height, and explain why *(2DS-B; L4)*
12. calculate the area of different triangles accurately *(2DS-B; L4)*
13. record, using words, a method for finding the area of any triangle *(2DS-B; L4)*
14. show the relationship between the number of cubes in one layer and the number of layers *(3DS-B; L5)*
15. explain that the volume of a rectangular prism equals the number of cubes in one layer multiplied by the number of layers *(3DS-B; L5)*

17. calculate volumes in cubic centimetres (cm³) *(3DS-B; L6)*

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·
*(sub-strand + content group + content point cited)*

### 🔴 RED — group A content (mandatory Red) + foundational single-step volume

**R1 — Products and factors**
*"I can multiply 2 or 3 numbers and call the answer the product, and I can list all the factors of a number."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(arrays / factor pairs)*
Covers SC 1, 2. *Multiplicative relations **A** (`MA3-MR-01`). Content points: "Use the term product to describe the result of multiplying 2 or more numbers"; "Determine factors for a given whole number."*

**R2 — Prime, composite or neither**
*"I can decide whether a number is prime, composite or neither (0 and 1), and explain why."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 3. *Multiplicative relations **A** (`MA3-MR-01`). Content point: "Determine whether a number is prime, composite or neither (0 or 1)."*

**R3 — The area model for multiplication**
*"I can use the area model (partitioning and place value) to multiply a 2-digit number by a one-digit number."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(grid paper / partitioned rectangle)*
Covers SC 4. *Multiplicative relations **A** (`MA3-MR-01`). Content points: "Use partitioning and place value to multiply 2-, 3- and 4-digit numbers by one-digit numbers"; "Use informal written strategies such as the area model to solve multiplication problems."*

**R4 — Area of rectangles in metric units**
*"I can calculate the area of a rectangle in square metres (m²), and also in cm² and km²."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(measure real rectangles)*
Covers SC 5. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-2DS-02`). Content point: "Calculate areas of rectangles in square centimetres (cm²), square metres (m²) and square kilometres (km²)."*

**R5 — Same perimeter, different dimensions**
*"I can show that rectangles with the same perimeter can have different dimensions and different areas, and compare them."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(grid-paper exploration)*
Covers SC 6, 7. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-02`) / Two-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-2DS-02`). Content points: "Recognise that rectangles with the same perimeter may have different dimensions"; "Investigate and compare the areas of rectangles that have the same perimeter."*

**R6 — Volume of rectangular prisms (layers, cm³)**
*"I can find the volume of a rectangular prism by working out the cubes in one layer × the number of layers, and calculate it in cubic centimetres (cm³)."*
Band: **Red** *(group B, but foundational/single-step → Red)* · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(centicubes / stacked layers)*
Covers SC 14, 15, 16, 17. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-3DS-02`). Content points: "Establish the relationship between the number of cubes in one layer and the number of layers to find the volume of a rectangular prism"; "Record, using words, the method for finding the volumes of rectangular prisms"; "Calculate volumes of rectangular prisms in cubic centimetres (cm³)."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying / multi-step (group B)

**Y1 — Area of a parallelogram**
*"I can turn a parallelogram into a rectangle by cutting and moving a piece, and calculate its area using base × perpendicular height."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(cut and rearrange paper parallelograms)*
Covers SC 8, 9, 10. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-02`, `MA3-2DS-03`). Content points: "Show how to transform a parallelogram into a rectangle to find its area"; "Record, using words, a method for finding the area of any parallelogram."*

**Y2 — Area of a triangle**
*"I know a triangle is half a parallelogram with the same base and height, and I can calculate the area of a triangle (½ × base × height)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(halve a parallelogram)*
Covers SC 11, 12, 13. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-02`). Content points: "Investigate the area of a triangle by comparing it to the area of a parallelogram with the same base length and height"; "Record, using words, a method for finding the area of any triangle."*

**Y3 — Volume in cubic metres**
*"I can explain a cubic metre as a measure of volume and calculate the volume of a rectangular prism in cubic metres (m³)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(build/estimate a cubic metre)*
Covers SC 18, 19, 21. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-3DS-02`). Content points: "Estimate and measure volumes in cubic metres"; "Calculate volumes of rectangular prisms in cubic centimetres (cm³) and cubic metres (m³)."*

**Y4 — Efficient multi-digit multiplication**
*"I can choose and use an efficient strategy to multiply a number up to 4 digits by a one- or 2-digit number."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 20. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). Content point: "Select and use efficient strategies to multiply whole numbers of up to 4 digits by one- and 2-digit numbers."*

**Y5 — Solve problems involving volume**
*"I can solve word problems about the volume of rectangular prisms, and show that prisms with the same volume can have different dimensions."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 21, 22. *Three-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-3DS-02`). Content points: "Calculate volumes of rectangular prisms in cubic centimetres (cm³) and cubic metres (m³)" (applied to problems); "Recognise that rectangular prisms with the same volume may have different dimensions."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 Core (verified against NESA K–10 2022)

No DoE Unit 37 lesson teaches these — expected for Green. Green extends the area work into Stage 4 **Area and
surface area** (`MA4-ARE-C-01`), the volume work into Stage 4 **Volume** (`MA4-VOL-C-01`), and the
products/factors work into Stage 4 **Indices** (`MA4-IND-C-01`).

**G1 — Area formulas and composite figures**
*"I can use the formulas A = bh (parallelogram) and A = ½bh (triangle), and find the area of a composite shape by splitting it into rectangles and triangles."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y1, Y2. *Stage 4 — **Area and surface area** (`MA4-ARE-C-01`). Content points: "Develop and apply the formula to find the area of a triangle: A = ½bh"; "Develop and apply the formula to find the area of a parallelogram: A = bh"; "Calculate the area of composite figures that can be dissected into rectangles, squares, parallelograms or triangles to solve problems."*

**G2 — Volume of prisms using V = base area × height**
*"I can use V = base area × height (V = Ah) to find the volume of a prism with a uniform cross-section, including triangular prisms."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R6, Y3, Y5. *Stage 4 — **Volume** (`MA4-VOL-C-01`). Content points: "Examine the idea that prisms have a uniform cross-section that is equal to the base area"; "Develop the formula for the volume of a prism: V = base area × height, leading to V = Ah"; "Apply the formula for the volume of a prism to prisms with uniform cross-sections to solve problems."*

**G3 — Prime factorisation and divisibility tests**
*"I can use divisibility tests, and write a whole number as a product of its prime factors using index notation (e.g. 72 = 2³ × 3²)."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R1, R2. *Stage 4 — **Indices** (`MA4-IND-C-01`). Content points: "Determine and apply tests for divisibility for 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 10"; "Represent a whole number greater than one as a product of its prime factors, using index notation where appropriate"; "Represent numbers in index notation limited to positive powers."*
