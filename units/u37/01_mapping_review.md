  > "Determine factors for a given whole number"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1**.

### R2 — Prime, composite or neither
- **NESA:** `MA3-MR-01` (Multiplicative relations **A**):
  > "Determine whether a number is prime, composite or neither (0 or 1)"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1**.

### R3 — The area model for multiplication
- **NESA:** `MA3-MR-01` (Multiplicative relations **A**):
  > "Use partitioning and place value to multiply 2-, 3- and 4-digit numbers by one-digit numbers"
  > "Use informal written strategies such as the area model to solve multiplication and division problems"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1**.

### R4 — Area of rectangles in metric units
- **NESA:** `MA3-2DS-02` (Two-dimensional spatial structure **A** — Area):
  > "Calculate areas of rectangles in square centimetres (cm²), square metres (m²) and square kilometres (km²)"
- **DoE lesson:** **L2**.

### R5 — Same perimeter, different dimensions
- **NESA:** `MA3-GM-02` (Geometric measure **A** — Length) / `MA3-2DS-02` (Two-dimensional spatial structure **A** — Area):
  > "Recognise that rectangles with the same perimeter may have different dimensions" *(GM-A, Spatial reasoning)*
  > "Investigate and compare the areas of rectangles that have the same perimeter" *(2DS-A)*
- **DoE lesson:** **L2**.

### R6 — Volume of rectangular prisms (layers, cm³)
- **NESA:** `MA3-3DS-02` (Three-dimensional spatial structure **B** — Volume):
  > "Establish the relationship between the number of cubes in one layer and the number of layers to find the volume of a rectangular prism" *(Reasons about spatial structure)*
  > "Record, using words, the method for finding the volumes of rectangular prisms"
  > "Calculate volumes of rectangular prisms in cubic centimetres (cm³) and cubic metres (m³)" *(cm³ component)*
- **DoE lesson:** **L5, L6**.

## 🟢 Green — Stage 4 Core (no DoE Unit 37 lesson — expected)

### G1 — Area formulas and composite figures
- **NESA:** `MA4-ARE-C-01` (Stage 4 Area and surface area):
  > "Develop and apply the formula to find the area of a triangle: A = ½bh, where b is the base length and h is the perpendicular height"
  > "Develop and apply the formula to find the area of a parallelogram: A = bh where b is the base length and h is the perpendicular height"
  > "Calculate the area of composite figures that can be dissected into rectangles, squares, parallelograms or triangles to solve problems"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original). Extends Y1, Y2.

### G2 — Volume of prisms using V = base area × height
- **NESA:** `MA4-VOL-C-01` (Stage 4 Volume):
  > "Examine the idea that prisms have a uniform cross-section that is equal to the base area"
  > "Develop the formula for the volume of a prism: V = base area × height, leading to V = Ah"
  > "Apply the formula for the volume of a prism to prisms with uniform cross-sections to solve problems"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original). Extends R6, Y3, Y5.

### G3 — Prime factorisation and divisibility tests
- **NESA:** `MA4-IND-C-01` (Stage 4 Indices):
  > "Determine and apply tests for divisibility for 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 10"
  > "Represent a whole number greater than one as a product of its prime factors, using index notation where appropriate"
  > "Represent numbers in index notation limited to positive powers"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original). Extends R1, R2.
