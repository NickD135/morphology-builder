## 🔴 Red

### R1 — Find 10%, 25% and 50% of a quantity
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-03` (Represents numbers **B** — percentages) — *Determine percentages of quantities*:
  > "Recognise that 10% is one-tenth of 100% and use this to find 10% of a quantity"
  > "Equate 10% to dividing by 10, 25% to finding a quarter by dividing by 4, and 50% to finding half"
- **DoE lesson:** **L4, L5** (Core concept).

### R2 — Thousandths and partitioning decimals
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-03` (Represents numbers **B** — decimals/place value) — *Recognise that the place value system can be extended beyond hundredths*:
  > "Express thousandths as decimals"
  > "Use place value to partition decimals"
- **DoE lesson:** **L3** (Core concept).

### R3 — Add and subtract decimals (to 3 dp)
- **NESA:** `MA3-AR-01` (Additive relations **B**) — *Apply known strategies to add and subtract decimals*:
  > "Model the addition and subtraction of decimals up to 3 decimal places using appropriate representations"
- **DoE lesson:** **L3, L4** (Core concept; daily number sense).

### R4 — Calculate elapsed time
- **NESA:** `MA3-NSM-02` (Non-spatial measure **B** — Time) — *Compare and convert 12- and 24-hour time; solve problems involving duration*:
  > "Use start and finish times to calculate the elapsed time of events"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1, L7, L8** (Core concept).

- **NESA:** `MA3-AR-01` (Additive relations **B**):

### Y2 — Choose an efficient add/subtract strategy
- **NESA:** `MA3-AR-01` (Additive relations **B**):

- **NESA:** `MA3-AR-01` (Additive relations **B**):

### Y4 — Percentages of quantities and discounts
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-03` (Represents numbers **B** — percentages):
  > "Represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals"
  > "Use mental strategies to estimate discounts of 10%, 25% and 50%"
- **DoE lesson:** **L5, L6** (Core concept).

### Y5 — Estimate and check reasonableness
- **NESA:** `MA3-AR-01` (Additive relations **B**) — *Use estimation and place value understanding to determine the reasonableness of solutions*:

### Y6 — Multi-step word problems
