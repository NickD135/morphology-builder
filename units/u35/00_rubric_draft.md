# Unit 35 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 Year B — Unit 35
**Topic:** *Addition and subtraction problems can be solved using a variety of strategies* — efficient
mental/written **add & subtract** of large numbers and **decimals to 3 dp**, **percentages of quantities**
and **discounts** (10%, 25%, 50%), **estimation/reasonableness**, **elapsed time/duration**, and
**multi-step word problems**.
**Source:** `DoE Unit 35.docx`, 8 lessons (Daily number sense / Core concept LI-SC template with
"Too hard? / Too easy?" differentiation — the core-concept SC are the unit content).
**Strands / outcomes:**
- `MA3-AR-01` — *selects and applies appropriate strategies to solve addition and subtraction problems* (Lessons 1–3, 6–8)
- `MA3-RN-03` — *determines percentages of quantities, and finds equivalent fractions and decimals for benchmark percentage values* (Lessons 4–6)
- `MA3-MR-02` — *constructs and completes number sentences, applying the order of operations* (multi-step; daily warm-ups)
- `MA3-NSM-02` — *measures and compares duration, using 12- and 24-hour time and am and pm notation* (elapsed time; Lessons 1, 7–8)
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **Multi-strand unit.** Like Unit 34, Unit 35 weaves several Stage 3 sub-strands (additive relations,
> decimals/percentages, time) around one big idea — *choosing efficient strategies for addition and
> subtraction*. Outcomes are grouped by skill, not strand, each carrying its sub-strand + content-group letter.

## 0a. Flat pool of extracted success criteria ("Students can …")

Extracted verbatim from each lesson's **Core concept** LI/SC tables, de-duplicated. The **daily-number-sense
inverse-operations / number-sentence warm-ups** (`MA3-MR-02`: "identify and use inverse operations…",
"complete number sentences with more than one operation") are **excluded as non-core** — they are revisited
MR-02 content already taught as full outcomes in Units 31/33, not the focus of this unit (same call as
Unit 33's excluded fraction warm-ups).

1. use place value to add or subtract 3 or more numbers with different numbers of digits *(L1, L2)*
2. determine when it would be more efficient to use a calculator to add numbers *(L1)*
3. compare, evaluate and communicate strategies used to solve addition and subtraction problems *(L1, L2, L7, L8)*
4. use start and finish times to calculate the elapsed time of events *(L1, L7, L8)*
5. apply known strategies such as levelling, addition for subtraction, using constant difference, and bridging *(L2)*
6. express thousandths as decimals *(L3)*
7. use place value to partition decimals *(L3)*
8. model the addition and subtraction of decimals up to 3 decimal places using appropriate representations *(L3, L4)*
9. justify why the strategy used to solve addition and subtraction word problems is appropriate *(L3)*
10. recognise that 10% is one-tenth of 100% and use this to find 10% of a quantity *(L4, L5)*
11. equate 10% to dividing by 10, 25% to finding a quarter by dividing by 4, and 50% to finding half *(L4, L5)*
12. represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals *(L5)*
13. round numbers appropriately when obtaining estimates to numerical calculations *(L6)*
14. use estimation to check the reasonableness of solutions to addition and subtraction calculations *(L6)*
15. use mental strategies to estimate discounts of 10%, 25% and 50% *(L6)*
16. solve word problems involving the addition and subtraction of decimals up to 3 decimal places *(L6, L7, L8)*
17. solve multistep word problems, including problems that require more than one operation *(L7, L8)*

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·
*(sub-strand + content group + content point cited)*

**Band-rule note.** Like Units 33 and 34, Unit 35's core content is almost entirely content group **B**
(the new Year-6 add/subtract learning; the DoE unit's prior-learning section confirms group-A basics — the
10%/25%/50% benchmarks and decimal-add/sub modelling — were the prior year). A strict A→Red/B→Yellow split
is unusable, so Unit 35 is banded by **SOLO cognitive demand** (single-step recall/procedure → Red;
applying / multi-step / word problems → Yellow), with the content-group letter shown per outcome.
Green = Stage 4 A.

### 🔴 RED — recall & single procedures

**R1 — Find 10%, 25% and 50% of a quantity**
*"I can find 10% (÷10), 25% (÷4) and 50% (half) of a quantity, and I know 10% is one-tenth of 100%."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 10, 11. *Represents numbers **B** — percentages (`MA3-RN-03`). Content points: "Recognise that 10% is one-tenth of 100% and use this to find 10% of a quantity"; "Equate 10% to dividing by 10, 25% to finding a quarter by dividing by 4, and 50% to finding half."*

**R2 — Thousandths and partitioning decimals**
*"I can express thousandths as decimals and use place value to partition a decimal (e.g. 2.456 = 2 + 0.4 + 0.05 + 0.006)."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(place-value chart)*
Covers SC 6, 7. *Represents numbers **B** — decimals (`MA3-RN-03` / place value). Content points: "Express thousandths as decimals"; "Use place value to partition decimals."*

**R3 — Add and subtract decimals (to 3 dp)**
*"I can add and subtract decimals up to 3 decimal places by lining up the place values."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(place-value chart)*
Covers SC 8. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). Content point: "Model the addition and subtraction of decimals up to 3 decimal places using appropriate representations."*

**R4 — Calculate elapsed time**
*"I can use start and finish times to work out how long an event lasts (its elapsed time)."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(clock face / time number line)*
Covers SC 4. *Non-spatial measure **B** — Time (`MA3-NSM-02`). Content point: "Use start and finish times to calculate the elapsed time of events."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying, multi-step & word problems

**Y1 — Add and subtract large numbers efficiently**
*"I can use place value to add or subtract 3 or more numbers with different numbers of digits, and decide when a calculator is the most efficient tool."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 1, 2, 3. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). Content points: "Use place value to add or subtract 3 or more numbers with different numbers of digits"; "Determine when it would be more efficient to use a calculator"; "Compare, evaluate and communicate strategies."*

**Y2 — Choose an efficient add/subtract strategy**
*"I can apply known strategies — levelling, addition for subtraction, constant difference and bridging — and pick an efficient one."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 5. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). Content point: "Apply known strategies such as levelling, addition for subtraction, using constant difference, and bridging."*

**Y3 — Decimal word problems (justify the strategy)**
*"I can solve word problems involving adding and subtracting decimals to 3 dp, and justify why my strategy is appropriate."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 9, 16. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). Content points: "Solve word problems involving the addition and subtraction of decimals up to 3 decimal places"; "Justify why the strategy used is appropriate."*

**Y4 — Percentages of quantities and discounts**
*"I can represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals, and find discounts of 10%, 25% and 50%."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 12, 15. *Represents numbers **B** — percentages (`MA3-RN-03`). Content points: "Represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals"; "Use mental strategies to estimate discounts of 10%, 25% and 50%."*

**Y5 — Estimate and check reasonableness**
*"I can round numbers to make an estimate and use it to check whether an answer is reasonable."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 13, 14. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). Content points: "Round numbers appropriately when obtaining estimates"; "Use estimation to check the reasonableness of solutions."*

**Y6 — Multi-step word problems**
*"I can solve multi-step word problems that need more than one operation, choosing efficient strategies."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 17. *Additive relations **B** / Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01` / `MA3-MR-02`). Content point: "Solve multistep word problems, including problems that require more than one operation."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 group A (verified against NESA K-10)

No DoE Unit 35 lesson teaches these — expected for Green. Green extends the unit's **percentage** work into
Stage 4 **Fractions, decimals and percentages** (`MA4-FRC-C-01`): finding any percentage of a quantity,
percentage increase/decrease and financial applications, and the reverse (finding the whole from a percentage).

**G1 — Find any percentage of a quantity**
*"I can find any percentage of a quantity (e.g. 15%, 12.5%), not just the 10/25/50% benchmarks."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R1/Y4. *Stage 4 — **Fractions, decimals and percentages** (`MA4-FRC-C-01`). Content point: "Apply knowledge of percentages to calculate quantities in various contexts."*

**G2 — Percentage increase and decrease (incl. GST and discounts)**
*"I can calculate a percentage increase or decrease — including discounts, mark-ups and 10% GST — to find a new price."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y4. *Stage 4 — **Fractions, decimals and percentages** (`MA4-FRC-C-01`). Content points: "Calculate percentage increases and decreases in various contexts"; "Solve financial problems involving percentages, specifically considering GST, profit and loss."*

**G3 — Find the whole from a percentage (reverse percentage)**
*"I can find the original (whole) amount when I am given a percentage of it, using the unitary method."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R4-style reasoning into percentages. *Stage 4 — **Fractions, decimals and percentages** (`MA4-FRC-C-01`). Content point: "Solve real-life problems involving percentages using the unitary method or other techniques."*
