# Unit 40 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 Year B — Unit 40
**Topic:** **Integers, Decimals, Percentages & the Cartesian Plane** — comparing/ordering decimals (to 3 dp,
trailing zeros, number line); integers on a number line (negatives, from subtraction); benchmark
fraction–decimal–percentage connections and finding 10%; percentage discounts (10%, 25%, 50%); adding and
subtracting decimals and multistep word problems; multiplying and dividing decimals (by powers of 10, mental
strategies, estimation); brackets and the order of operations; and the Cartesian coordinate system (grid-map
vs number plane, coordinates in all four quadrants).
**Source:** `DoE Unit 40.zip` → `Mathematics-Stage3-Unit40.docx`, 8 lessons (per-lesson "Syllabus — Learning
intentions and success criteria" tables with sub-strand label + A/B group).
**Strands / outcomes (from the SC tables + the "Outcomes and content" grid, Table 26):**
- `MA3-RN-01` — *applies an understanding of place value and the role of zero to represent... whole numbers and decimals* — content groups **A** (decimals) and **B** (integers, discounts)
- `MA3-RN-03` — *determines percentages of quantities, and finds equivalent fractions and decimals for benchmark percentage values* — content group **B**
- `MA3-AR-01` — *selects and applies appropriate strategies to solve addition and subtraction problems* — content group **B**
- `MA3-MR-01` / `MA3-MR-02` — *multiplication/division strategies; constructs and completes number sentences* — content group **B**
- `MA3-GM-01` — *locates and describes points on a coordinate plane* — content groups **A** (Cartesian system) and **B** (4 quadrants)
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **A/B read from the DoE "Outcomes and content" grid (Table 26), not from difficulty.** The grid puts
> **Represents numbers A** (compare/order decimals; trailing zeros; approximate decimals; decimals on a
> number line) and **Geometric measure A** (the Cartesian system: grid-map vs number plane) in **group A**;
> and **Represents numbers B** (integers; FDP benchmarks & 10%; percentage discounts), **Additive relations B**
> (add/subtract decimals; multistep word problems), **Multiplicative relations B** (×/÷ decimals by powers of
> 10; order of operations) and **Geometric measure B** (coordinates in all 4 quadrants) in **group B**.
>
> **Banding (policy set 2026-06-20).** Every **group A** content point goes in **Red** (mandatory) — so R1
> (decimals) and the Cartesian-system part of R5 are group A. Group B is then banded by **SOLO cognitive
> demand**: the foundational, single-step B work (placing integers; benchmark FDP & finding 10%; ×/÷ decimals
> by powers of 10; reading/recording coordinates) sits in **Red** (simple B in Red is allowed); the
> applying/multi-step B work (percentage discounts, add/subtract decimal word problems, multistep problems,
> multiplying decimals with estimation, order of operations) sits in **Yellow**. Green = Stage 4 **Core**.

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·
*(sub-strand + content group + content point cited)*

### 🔴 RED — group A content (mandatory Red) + foundational single-step B

**R1 — Compare and order decimals**
*"I can compare and order decimals to 3 decimal places on a number line, read trailing zeros, and estimate the size of a decimal."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(decimal number line)*
Covers SC 3, 4, 5. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-01`). Content points: "Compare and order decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places"; "Interpret zero digit(s) at the end of a decimal"; "Approximate the size of decimals"; "Place decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places on a number line."*

**R2 — Integers on a number line**
*"I can place negative whole numbers on a number line and explain that subtraction can give a negative answer."*
Band: **Red** *(group B, but foundational/single-step → Red)* · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(integer number line)*
Covers SC 1, 2. *Represents numbers **B** (`MA3-RN-01`). Content points: "Recognise the location of negative whole numbers in relation to zero and place them on a number line"; "Recognise that negative whole numbers can result from subtraction."*

**R3 — Benchmark fractions, decimals, percentages & 10%**
*"I can write common percentages as fractions and decimals, and find 10% of an amount (one-tenth)."*
Band: **Red** *(group B, but foundational → Red)* · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 9, 10. *Represents numbers **B** (`MA3-RN-03`). Content points: "Represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals"; "Recognise that 10% is one-tenth of 100% and use this to find 10% of a quantity."*

**R4 — Multiply and divide decimals by powers of 10**
*"I can use place value to multiply and divide a decimal by 10, 100 and 1000."*
Band: **Red** *(group B, but single-step place-value rule → Red)* · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 13. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). Content point: "Compare the relative place value of digits to multiply and divide a decimal by powers of 10."*

**R5 — The Cartesian coordinate system**
*"I can read and record the coordinates of points in all four quadrants, and explain how a number plane locates an exact point."*
Band: **Red** *(GM-A mandatory + GM-B coordinates as simple B)* · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(coordinate grid)*
Covers SC 17, 18. *Geometric measure **A** & **B** (`MA3-GM-01`). Content points: "Recognise that the grid-map reference system gives the area of a location and the number plane identifies a specific point" (A); "Identify and record the coordinates of given points on the number plane in all 4 quadrants" (B).*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying / multi-step (group B)

**Y1 — Percentage discounts**
*"I can estimate a 10%, 25% or 50% discount and calculate the sale price of an item."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 11, 12. *Represents numbers **B** (`MA3-RN-03`). Content points: "Use mental strategies to estimate discounts of 10%, 25% and 50%"; "Calculate the sale price of an item after a discount of 10%, 25% and 50%."*

**Y2 — Add and subtract decimals**
*"I can add and subtract decimals to 3 decimal places to solve word problems, and explain why my strategy works."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 6, 8. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). Content points: "Solve word problems involving the addition and subtraction of decimals up to 3 decimal places"; "Justify why the strategy used to solve addition and subtraction word problems is appropriate."*

**Y3 — Multistep word problems**
*"I can solve multistep word problems that need more than one operation."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 7. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). Content point: "Solve multistep word problems, including problems that require more than one operation."*

**Y4 — Multiply decimals (mental strategies & estimation)**
*"I can multiply a decimal by a single-digit number using mental strategies, and estimate the product first."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 14, 15. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). Content points: "Use mental strategies to multiply benchmark decimals by single-digit numbers"; "Estimate the product of a decimal and a whole number to determine the magnitude of a calculator answer."*

**Y5 — Order of operations and brackets**
*"I can use brackets and the order of operations to solve number problems."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 16. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`, `MA3-MR-02`). Content points: "Recognise the need to agree on the order in which to perform operations"; "Solve problems involving grouping symbols."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 Core (verified against NESA K–10 2022)

No DoE Unit 40 lesson teaches these — expected for Green. Green extends the integer work into Stage 4

**G1 — Add, subtract and order integers**
*"I can compare and order positive and negative integers, and add and subtract them."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R2. *Stage 4 — **Computation with integers** (`MA4-INT-C-01`). Content points: "Compare and order integers"; "Compare the relative value of integers using < and >"; "Add and subtract positive and negative integers."*

**G2 — Percentages of quantities and percentage change**
*"I can find any percentage of a quantity and work out a percentage increase or decrease."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R3, Y1. *Stage 4 — **Fractions, decimals and percentages** (`MA4-FRC-C-01`). Content points: "Find percentages of quantities"; "Solve problems involving percentage increase and decrease."*

**G3 — Plot points and linear relationships on the Cartesian plane**
*"I can plot points in all four quadrants and plot a simple linear relationship from a table of values."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R5. *Stage 4 — **Linear relationships** (`MA4-LIN-C-01`). Content points: "Plot and label points on the Cartesian plane of given coordinates"; "Plot linear relationships on the Cartesian plane."*
