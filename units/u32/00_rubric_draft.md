**2D shapes (Lessons 1–3, 2DS-01):**
1. investigate the line and rotational symmetry properties of quadrilaterals *(L1)*
2. identify regular and irregular polygons *(L1)*
3. use the terms 'translate', 'reflect' and 'rotate' to describe transformations of two-dimensional shapes *(L2, L3)*
4. recognise that translations, reflections or rotations change the position and orientation but not the size of shapes *(L2, L4)*
5. dissect and rearrange one shape to make another *(L3)*

**Factors (Lesson 5, MR-01):**
6. determine factors for a given whole number *(L5)*

**Area (Lessons 4–8, 2DS-02 / 2DS-03):**
7. find different ways to calculate the area of a composite L-shape figure *(L4, L5)*
8. show how to transform a parallelogram into a rectangle to find its area *(L6)*
9. record, using words, a method for finding the area of any parallelogram *(L6)*
10. investigate the area of a triangle by comparing it to the area of a parallelogram with the same base length and height *(L7)*
11. establish the relationship between the area of a triangle and the area of a parallelogram formed by duplicating and rotating the triangle *(L8)*
12. record, using words, a method for finding the area of any triangle *(L8)*

### ⚠️ Band-rule note for Unit 32 (read before the gate)

Unit 32 is the **mirror image of Unit 29**: where Unit 29 was almost all content group **A**, Unit 32 is
almost all content group **B** (transformations, composite area, parallelogram & triangle area). The only
group-**A** content is *symmetry of quadrilaterals / regular & irregular polygons* (`MA3-2DS-01` A) and a
single *factors* content point (`MA3-MR-01` A). A strict A→Red / B→Yellow split would give ~2 Red / 7
Yellow — no usable SOLO progression.

Per spec §0b ("confirm per unit against the DoE unit's own framing… a reliable starting point, not a
trap"), Unit 32 is banded by **SOLO cognitive demand** within the Stage 3 content, with the syllabus
content-group letter shown against every outcome:
- **Red** = recall / identify / vocabulary (symmetry, polygon classification, transformation *terms* &
  the recognition that transformations preserve size, factors).
- **Yellow** = applying & spatial reasoning (dissect & rearrange, and the area-by-rearrangement work).
- **Green** = Stage 4 Area formulas (`MA4-ARE-C-01`).

This is the one Stage-0 judgement call for Nick to confirm/override.

### 🔴 RED — recall, identify & vocabulary

**R1 — Line and rotational symmetry of quadrilaterals**
*"I can find the lines of symmetry of a quadrilateral and work out its order of rotational symmetry."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(fold paper / trace and turn shapes)*
Covers SC 1. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-2DS-01`). 2D shapes: Classify two-dimensional shapes and describe their properties — content point: "Investigate the symmetry properties (line and rotational) of quadrilaterals."*

**R2 — Identify regular and irregular polygons**
*"I can identify a polygon as regular (all sides and angles equal) or irregular."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 2. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **A** (`MA3-2DS-01`). content point: "Identify regular and irregular polygons."*

**R3 — Describe translations, reflections and rotations**
*"I can use the terms translate, reflect and rotate to describe how a shape has moved, and I know these transformations change a shape's position and orientation but not its size."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(slide, flip and turn cut-out shapes)*
Covers SC 3, 4. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-01`). 2D shapes: Dissect two-dimensional shapes and rearrange them — content points: "Use the terms translate, reflect and rotate to describe transformations of two-dimensional shapes"; "Recognise that translations, reflections or rotations change the position and orientation but not the size of shapes (Reasons about spatial orientation)."*

**R4 — Determine the factors of a whole number**
*"I can find all the factors of a given whole number."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 6. *Multiplicative relations **A** (`MA3-MR-01`). Determine products and factors — content point: "Determine factors for a given whole number." (Supports flexible dissection of composite areas.)*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying & spatial reasoning

**Y1 — Dissect and rearrange one shape to make another**
*"I can dissect (cut up) a shape and rearrange the pieces using translations, reflections and rotations to make a different shape."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(cut and rearrange shapes)*
Covers SC 5. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-01`). content point: "Dissect and rearrange one shape to make another."*

**Y2 — Find the area of a composite (L-shape) figure**
*"I can find the area of a composite L-shape figure in more than one way, by splitting it into rectangles (or surrounding and subtracting)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(grid paper; split / surround the L-shape)*
Covers SC 7. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-02` / `MA3-2DS-03`). Area: Find the area of composite figures — content point: "Find different ways to calculate the area of a composite L-shape figure."*

**Y3 — Find the area of a parallelogram by rearrangement**
*"I can show how to transform a parallelogram into a rectangle to find its area, and record a method for finding the area of any parallelogram."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(cut the parallelogram and rearrange into a rectangle)*
Covers SC 8, 9. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-03`). Area: Calculate the area of a parallelogram using subdivision and rearrangement — content points: "Show how to transform a parallelogram into a rectangle to find its area"; "Record, using words, a method for finding the area of any parallelogram."*

**Y4 — Compare a triangle's area to a parallelogram's**
*"I can investigate the area of a triangle by comparing it to a parallelogram with the same base length and height."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(match a triangle against a same-base/height parallelogram)*
Covers SC 10. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-03`). Area: Determine the area of a triangle — content point: "Investigate the area of a triangle by comparing it to the area of a parallelogram with the same base length and height."*

**Y5 — Establish and record the area-of-a-triangle method**
*"I can establish that a triangle is half a parallelogram (formed by duplicating and rotating the triangle), and record a method for finding the area of any triangle."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(duplicate & rotate a triangle to build a parallelogram)*
Covers SC 11, 12. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-03`). content points: "Establish the relationship between the area of a triangle and the area of a parallelogram formed by duplicating and rotating the triangle"; "Record, using words, a method for finding the area of any triangle."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 group A (Area — verified against NESA K-10)

No DoE Unit 32 lesson teaches these — expected for Green. All use **Stage 4 group A** content from the
**Area** focus area (`MA4-ARE-C-01` — *applies knowledge of area and composite area… to solve
problems*). This is the genuine Stage-4 generalisation: where Stage 3 finds area by *rearrangement*,
Stage 4 develops and applies the **formulas**.

**G1 — Area of a rectangle and a parallelogram by formula**
*"I can apply the formula for the area of a rectangle (A = lb) and develop and apply the formula for the area of a parallelogram (A = bh, base × perpendicular height)."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y3. *Stage 4 — **Area** (`MA4-ARE-C-01`). Content points: "Apply the formula to find the area of a rectangle or square: A = lb, where l is the length and b is the breadth (or width) of the rectangle or square"; "Develop and apply the formula to find the area of a parallelogram: A = bh where b is the base length and h is the perpendicular height."*

**G2 — Area of a triangle by formula**
*"I can develop and apply the formula for the area of a triangle: A = ½bh (half base × perpendicular height)."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y4/Y5. *Stage 4 — **Area** (`MA4-ARE-C-01`). Content point: "Develop and apply the formula to find the area of a triangle: A = ½bh, where b is the base length and h is the perpendicular height."*

**G3 — Area of composite figures by dissection (formulas)**
*"I can calculate the area of a composite figure by dissecting it into rectangles, squares, parallelograms or triangles and applying the area formulas."*
Band: **Green** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(dissect composite shapes on grid paper)*
Extends Y2. *Stage 4 — **Area** (`MA4-ARE-C-01`). Content point: "Calculate the area of composite figures that can be dissected into rectangles, squares, parallelograms or triangles to solve problems."*
