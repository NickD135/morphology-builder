# Unit 21 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 **Year B** — Unit 21
**Topic:** **Number: Place Value, Decimals & Powers of 10** — reading and ordering numbers in the millions
(to 1 billion); partitioning and regrouping in standard and non-standard forms; comparing and ordering
decimals to 3 dp (trailing zeros, approximating, number line); finding numbers 10/100 times (and one-tenth/
one-hundredth of) a decimal; multiplying by 10, 100 and 1000 mentally; adding and subtracting decimals to
3 dp; and estimating products to check the reasonableness of answers.
**Source:** `DoE Unit 21.zip` → `Mathematics-Stage3-Unit21.docx`, 8 lessons.
**Year note:** This is a **Year B** unit (the *first* Year B / Year 6 unit). The "Year A" strings in the DoE
doc are **prior-learning cross-references** to earlier Year A units, not this unit's own year. The program
docx is correctly built as "Year B – Unit 21".
**Strands / outcomes (from the SC tables + the "Outcomes and content" grid, Table 20):**
- `MA3-RN-01` — *applies an understanding of place value and the role of zero to represent the properties of numbers* — content group **A** (whole numbers to 1 billion)
- `MA3-RN-02` — *compares and orders decimals up to 3 decimal places* — content group **A**
- `MA3-AR-01` — *selects and applies appropriate strategies to solve addition and subtraction problems* — content group **B** (the one B point: model add/subtract of decimals)
- `MA3-MR-01` — *selects and applies appropriate strategies to solve multiplication and division problems* — content group **A** (×10/100/1000; estimation)
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **Banding — cognitive-demand exception (set by Nick 2026-06-20, see `feedback_solo_band_a_heavy_units`).**
> Unit 21 is the **first Year B unit** and does a gradual release from Year 5 — so it is **overwhelmingly
> Group A content** (only one Group B point: model add/subtract of decimals). Applying the standard "every
> Group A content point → Red" hard rule would put ~everything in Red and leave Yellow empty. So for this
> unit we **band by SOLO cognitive demand** across both A and B: recall/single-step/foundational → **Red**;
> applying/multi-step/reasoning → **Yellow**. The A/B group label is still recorded on every content point in
> both deliverables (that rule is unchanged). Green = Stage 4 **Core**.

## 0a. Flat pool of extracted success criteria ("Students can …")

Extracted verbatim from each lesson's "Syllabus — Learning intentions and success criteria" table,
de-duplicated, with the sub-strand + A/B group noted.

1. arrange numbers in the millions in ascending and descending order using place value *(RN-A; L1)*
2. recognise 1000 thousands is 1 million and 1000 millions is 1 billion *(RN-A; L1)*
3. regroup numbers in different ways to show the same value *(RN-A; L2)*
4. partition numbers to 1 billion in non-standard forms *(RN-A; L2)*
5. compare and order decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places *(RN-A; L4, L7)*
6. explain that zero digit(s) at the end of a decimal does not change the value *(RN-A; L4)*
7. approximate the size of decimals *(RN-A; L4)*
8. place decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places on a number line *(RN-A; L4)*
9. compare the place value of digits by finding numbers that are 10 or 100 times (and one-tenth/one-hundredth of) the original decimal *(RN-A; L3, L5, L7)*
10. multiply one-digit numbers by 10, 100 and 1000 using mental strategies *(MR-A; L3, L5)*
11. add and subtract decimals up to 3 decimal places *(AR-B; L6)*
12. estimate the product of 2 numbers (one-digit by 2- or 3-digit) using multiples of 10 or 100 *(MR-A; L8)*
13. use estimation to check the reasonableness of answers to calculations *(MR-A; L8)*

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·

### 🔴 RED — recall / single-step / foundational

**R1 — Read and order numbers in the millions**
*"I can read, order and place-value numbers in the millions, and I know 1000 thousands = 1 million and 1000 millions = 1 billion."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(place value chart)*
Covers SC 1, 2. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-01`). Content points: "Arrange numbers in the millions in ascending and descending order using place value"; "Recognise 1000 thousands is 1 million and 1000 millions is 1 billion."*

**R2 — Compare and order decimals**
*"I can compare and order decimals to 3 decimal places, read trailing zeros, approximate a decimal's size and place decimals on a number line."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(decimal number line)*
Covers SC 5, 6, 7, 8. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-02`). Content points: "Compare and order decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places"; "Interpret zero digit(s) at the end of a decimal"; "Approximate the size of decimals"; "Place decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places on a number line."*

**R3 — Multiply by 10, 100 and 1000 (mental strategies)**
*"I can multiply one-digit numbers by 10, 100 and 1000 using mental strategies."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 10. *Multiplicative relations **A** (`MA3-MR-01`). Content point: "Use mental strategies to multiply one-digit numbers by 10, 100, 1000 and their multiples."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying / multi-step / reasoning

**Y1 — Partition and regroup numbers (non-standard forms)**
*"I can partition and regroup numbers to 1 billion in different (non-standard) ways that show the same value."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 3, 4. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-01`). Content points: "Regroup numbers in different forms"; "Partition numbers to 1 billion in non-standard forms."*

**Y2 — Place value: 10 and 100 times a decimal**
*"I can find the number that is 10 or 100 times a decimal, and one-tenth or one-hundredth of it, using place value."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 9. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-02`). Content point: "Compare the place value of digits by determining numbers that are 10 or 100 times the original decimal number as well as one-tenth or one-hundredth of the original."*

**Y3 — Add and subtract decimals**
*"I can add and subtract decimals up to 3 decimal places, lining up the decimal points."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(place value chart / number line)*
Covers SC 11. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). Content point: "Model the addition and subtraction of decimals up to 3 decimal places using appropriate representations."*

**Y4 — Estimate products and check reasonableness**
*"I can estimate the product of two numbers using multiples of 10 or 100, and use estimation to check whether an answer is reasonable."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 12, 13. *Multiplicative relations **A** (`MA3-MR-01`). Content points: "Estimate the product of 2 numbers (one-digit by 2- or 3-digit numbers) using multiples of 10 or 100"; "Use estimation to check the reasonableness of answers to calculations."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 Core (verified against NESA K–10 2022)

No DoE Unit 21 lesson teaches these — expected for Green. Green extends the place-value work into Stage 4
**Indices** (`MA4-IND-C-01`), the decimal work into **Fractions, decimals and percentages** (`MA4-FRC-C-01`),
and the estimation/calculation work into **Computation with integers** (`MA4-INT-C-01`).

**G1 — Powers of 10 and index notation**
*"I can write powers of 10 in index notation (10² = 100, 10⁶ = 1 000 000) and use them to write large numbers."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R1, R3. *Stage 4 — **Indices** (`MA4-IND-C-01`). Content points: "Represent numbers in index notation limited to positive powers"; "Represent in expanded form and evaluate numbers expressed in index notation, including powers of 10."*

**G2 — Multiply and divide decimals**
*"I can multiply and divide decimals (including by powers of 10) and round a decimal to a given number of places."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R2, R3, Y3. *Stage 4 — **Fractions, decimals and percentages** (`MA4-FRC-C-01`). Content points: "Multiply and divide decimals by powers of 10"; "Round decimals to a specified number of decimal places."*

**G3 — Order of operations with whole numbers and decimals**
*"I can use the order of operations (brackets, then × and ÷, then + and −) to evaluate expressions."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y4. *Stage 4 — **Computation with integers** (`MA4-INT-C-01`). Content points: "Apply the order of operations to evaluate expressions"; "Use estimation and rounding to check the reasonableness of answers."*
