# Unit 39 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 Year B — Unit 39
**Topic:** **Time, Transformations & Angles** — measuring, creating and classifying angles with a protractor;
angle relationships (right angles, angles on a straight line = 180°, angles at a point = 360°, perpendicular
lines); identifying angle types and parts in diagrams; transformations (translate, reflect, rotate) and
dissecting/rearranging shapes; calculating elapsed time (bridging, rounding); representing time intervals as
decimals; and solving duration problems in 12- and 24-hour time.
**Source:** `DoE Unit 39.zip` → `Mathematics-Stage3-Unit39.docx`, 8 lessons (per-lesson "Syllabus — Learning
intentions and success criteria" tables with sub-strand label + A/B group).
**Strands / outcomes (from the SC tables + the "Outcomes and content" grid, Table 18):**
- `MA3-GM-03` — *measures and constructs angles, and identifies the relationships between angles on a straight line and angles at a point* — content groups **A** (measure/create/classify angles) and **B** (angle relationships in diagrams)
- `MA3-2DS-01` — *investigates and classifies two-dimensional shapes, including triangles and quadrilaterals* — content group **B** (transformations / dissect & rearrange)
- `MA3-NSM-02` — *measures and compares duration, using 12- and 24-hour time* — content group **B**
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **A/B read from the DoE "Outcomes and content" grid (Table 18), not from difficulty.** The grid puts
> **Geometric measure A** (measure angles up to 360° with a protractor; create angles with a protractor;
> classify acute/obtuse/reflex) in **group A**; and **Geometric measure B** (angles in diagrams; vertex/arms;
> adjacent angles adding to 90°/180°/360°; perpendicular lines), **Two-dimensional spatial structure B**
> (translate/reflect/rotate; dissect & rearrange; orientation not size) and **Non-spatial measure B** (all
> time/duration) in **group B**.
>
> **Banding (policy set 2026-06-20).** Every **group A** content point goes in **Red** (mandatory) — so R1–R3
> are the GM-A angle skills. Group B is then banded by **SOLO cognitive demand**: the foundational angle
> facts (find a missing angle using 90°/180°/360°; perpendicular) sit in **Red** even though group B (simple
> B in Red is allowed); the applying/reasoning group-B work (interpreting angle diagrams, transformations,
> dissecting shapes, elapsed time, time as decimals, 24-hour duration problems) sits in **Yellow**.
> Green = Stage 4 **Core** content.

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·
*(sub-strand + content group + content point cited)*

### 🔴 RED — group A angle skills (mandatory Red) + foundational angle facts

**R1 — Classify angles**
*"I can name an angle as acute, right, obtuse, straight or reflex from its size in degrees."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 17. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-03`). Content point: "Identify and describe angle size in degrees for the classifications acute, obtuse and reflex."*

**R2 — Measure angles with a protractor**
*"I can use a protractor to measure angles of up to 360°."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-required]** *(protractor)*
Covers SC 16. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-03`). Content point: "Measure angles of up to 360° using a protractor."*

**R3 — Draw angles with a protractor**
*"I can use a protractor to draw (create) angles of up to 360°."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-required]** *(protractor)*
Covers SC 12. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-03`). Content point: "Create angles of up to 360° using a protractor."*

**R4 — Angles on a straight line and at a point**
*"I can find a missing angle using right angles (90°), angles on a straight line (180°), angles at a point (360°) and perpendicular lines."*
Band: **Red** *(group B, but foundational/single-step → Red)* · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 10, 11, 14, 15. *Geometric measure **B** (`MA3-GM-03`). Content points: "Recognise that perpendicular lines intersect at right angles (90°)"; "Investigate adjacent angles that form a right angle and establish that they add to 90°"; "Investigate adjacent angles on a straight line and establish that they add to 180°"; "Investigate angles at a point and establish that they add to 360°."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying / reasoning (group B angles, transformations & time)

**Y1 — Identify angles and their parts in diagrams**
*"I can find right angles, angles on a straight line and angles at a point in diagrams, and name the vertex and arms of an angle."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 9, 13, 18. *Geometric measure **B** (`MA3-GM-03`). Content points: "Recognise right angles, angles on a straight line and angles at a point embedded in diagrams"; "Identify the vertex and arms of angles formed by intersecting lines"; "Identify angle types formed by the intersection of straight lines."*

**Y2 — Describe transformations (translate, reflect, rotate)**
*"I can use the words translate, reflect and rotate to describe how a 2D shape has been moved."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(move/flip/turn paper shapes)*
Covers SC 6, 8. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-01`). Content points: "Use the terms translate, reflect and rotate to describe transformations"; "Recognise that translations, reflections or rotations change the position and orientation but not the size of shapes."*

**Y3 — Dissect and rearrange shapes**
*"I can cut one shape and rearrange the pieces to make another shape."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(cut & rearrange)*
Covers SC 7. *Two-dimensional spatial structure **B** (`MA3-2DS-01`). Content point: "Dissect and rearrange one shape to make another."*

**Y4 — Calculate elapsed time**
*"I can use start and finish times to work out how long something takes, using bridging, and round to the nearest minute or hour."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 1, 2, 3. *Non-spatial measure **B** (`MA3-NSM-02`). Content points: "Use start and finish times to calculate the elapsed time of events"; "Add and subtract time mentally using bridging strategies"; "Round answers to time calculations to the nearest minute or hour."*

**Y5 — Represent time intervals as decimals**
*"I can write commonly used time intervals as decimals (e.g. 30 min = 0.5 h, 15 min = 0.25 h)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 4. *Non-spatial measure **B** (`MA3-NSM-02`). Content point: "Represent commonly used time intervals as decimals."*

**Y6 — Solve duration problems in 12- and 24-hour time**
*"I can convert between 12-hour and 24-hour time and solve duration problems."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 5. *Non-spatial measure **B** (`MA3-NSM-02`). Content point: "Solve a variety of problems involving duration, including where times are expressed in 12-hour and 24-hour notation."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 Core (verified against NESA K–10 2022)

No DoE Unit 39 lesson teaches these — expected for Green. Green extends the angle work into Stage 4
**Angle relationships** (`MA4-ANG-C-01`).

**G1 — Complementary, supplementary and vertically opposite angles**
*"I can use complementary angles (add to 90°), supplementary angles (add to 180°) and vertically opposite angles (equal) to find unknown angles."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R4, Y1. *Stage 4 — **Angle relationships** (`MA4-ANG-C-01`). Content points: "Identify right angles, straight angles, angles of complete revolution and vertically opposite angles"; "Apply the terms complementary and supplementary to a pair of angles adding to 90° and 180°."*

**G2 — Angles on parallel lines cut by a transversal**
*"I can identify corresponding, alternate and co-interior angles when parallel lines are cut by a transversal, and use them to find angles."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y1. *Stage 4 — **Angle relationships** (`MA4-ANG-C-01`). Content points: "Identify and describe corresponding, alternate and co-interior angles when 2 straight lines are crossed by a transversal, including parallel lines"; "Verify… corresponding and alternate angles as equal, and co-interior angles as supplementary, when a pair of parallel lines is cut by a transversal."*

**G3 — Solve numerical angle problems using reasoning**
*"I can combine angle relationships to find unknown angles in a multi-step problem and give a reason."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R4, G1. *Stage 4 — **Angle relationships** (`MA4-ANG-C-01`). Content points: "Solve numerical problems involving angles using reasoning"; "Apply the knowledge of angle relationships including angles at a point to find the sizes of unknown angles."*
