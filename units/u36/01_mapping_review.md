## 🔴 Red

### R1 — The whole and different-sized wholes
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-02` (Representing quantity fractions **A**):
  > "Recognise the role of the number 1 as representing the whole"
  > "Compare halves and quarters of different-sized wholes"
  > "Justify the need for fractions to refer to the number 1 as the common whole"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1** (Core concept).

### R2 — Fraction, decimal and percentage equivalence
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-03` (Represents numbers **B** — decimals and percentages):
  > "Recognise that the symbol % means percent and 100% is the whole amount"
  > "Recall commonly used equivalent percentages, decimals and fractions"
- **DoE lesson:** **L2** (Core concept).

### R3 — Find fractions of whole numbers
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-02` / `MA3-RN-03`:
  > "Find fractional quantities of whole numbers (halves, quarters, fifths and tenths)"
  > "Represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals"
  > "Recognise that 10% is one-tenth of 100% and use this to find 10% of a quantity"
- **DoE lesson:** **L3, L5** (Core concept; tape diagram).

### R4 — Subtract a unit fraction from a whole (complement)
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-02` (Representing quantity fractions **A**):
  > "Use diagrams, objects and mental strategies to subtract a unit fraction from any whole number including 1 (the complement principle)"
- **DoE lesson:** **L6** (Core concept).

## 🟡 Yellow

### Y1 — Build the whole from a fractional part
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-02` (Representing quantity fractions **B**):
  > "Build up to the whole from a given fractional part"
  > "Generate the whole quantity from non-unit fractional parts"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1** (Core concept).

### Y2 — Compare fractions with related denominators
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-01` (Representing quantity fractions **B**):
  > "Compare and represent fractions with denominators of 2, 4 and 8; 3 and 6; 5 and 10 of a whole shape (area model) and a collection of objects (discrete model)"
  > "Subdivide the area of a rectangle by both length and width to represent the multiplicative relationship between common fractions"
- **DoE lesson:** **L4** (Core concept).

### Y3 — Order fractions with related denominators
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-01` (Representing quantity fractions **B**):
  > "Order common fractions with related denominators using diagrams and number lines"
- **DoE lesson:** **L5** (Core concept).

### Y4 — Add and subtract fractions with related denominators
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-02` (Representing quantity fractions **B**):
  > "Use equivalence to add and subtract fractional quantities"
  > "Represent fractional quantities with the same or related denominators to add and subtract fractions"
- **DoE lesson:** **L6, L7** (Core concept).

### Y5 — Solve fraction word problems
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-02` (Representing quantity fractions **B**):
  > "Solve word problems involving adding or subtracting fractional quantities with related denominators"
- **DoE lesson:** **L7** (Core concept).

### Y6 — Chance: probabilities with fractions and benchmark FDP
- **NESA:** `MA3-CHAN-01` (Chance **B**):

- **DoE lesson:** **L8** (Core concept).
