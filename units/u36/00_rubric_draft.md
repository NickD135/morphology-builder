# Unit 36 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 Year B — Unit 36
**Topic:** **Fractions** — the whole and different-sized wholes, benchmark fraction–decimal–percentage
equivalence, fractions of whole numbers, comparing/ordering fractions with related denominators (area &
number-line models), subtracting a unit fraction from a whole (complement principle), adding/subtracting
fractions with related denominators and fraction word problems — plus **chance** using fractions, decimals
and percentages.
**Source:** `DoE Unit 36.zip` → `Mathematics-Stage3-Unit36.docx`, 8 lessons (Daily number sense / Core
concept LI-SC template with "Too hard? / Too easy?" differentiation; **the SC tables explicitly label
"Representing quantity fractions A" and "B" content** — see band note).
**Strands / outcomes:**
- `MA3-RQF-01` — *compares and orders fractions with denominators of 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10* (Lessons 4–5)
- `MA3-RQF-02` — *determines fractions of measures and quantities; adds and subtracts fractions* (Lessons 1, 5–7)
- `MA3-RN-03` — *determines percentages of quantities, and finds equivalent fractions and decimals for benchmark percentage values* (Lessons 2–3)
- `MA3-CHAN-01` — *conducts chance experiments and quantifies probability* (Lesson 8)
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **A/B split is genuine here.** Unlike Units 33–35 (≈all content group B), Unit 36's DoE LI/SC tables
> explicitly carry **"Representing quantity fractions A"** content (recognise the whole; compare halves/
> quarters of different-sized wholes; subtract a unit fraction from a whole — the complement principle) and
> **"Representing quantity fractions B"** content (build the whole from a part; compare/order related
> denominators; equivalence to add/subtract; word problems). So the **A→Red / B→Yellow** mapping fits this
> unit cleanly and is supported by both the syllabus labels and SOLO cognitive demand. Chance B (assign
> probabilities) sits in Yellow (applying). Green = Stage 4 A.

## 0a. Flat pool of extracted success criteria ("Students can …")

Extracted verbatim from each lesson's Core-concept LI/SC tables, de-duplicated, with the A/B label noted.

1. recognise the role of the number 1 as representing the whole *(A; L1)*
2. compare halves and quarters of different-sized wholes; justify the need for a common whole *(A; L1)*
3. build up to the whole from a given fractional part (generate the whole from non-unit fractional parts) *(B; L1)*
4. recognise that % means percent and 100% is the whole amount *(RN-03; L2)*
5. recall commonly used equivalent percentages, decimals and fractions (½ = 50% = 0.5, ¼ = 25%, ¾ = 75%, etc.) *(RN-03; L2)*
6. represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals; recognise 10% is one-tenth of 100% and find 10% of a quantity *(RN-03; L3)*
7. find fractional quantities of whole numbers (halves, quarters, fifths and tenths) *(B; L3, L5)*
8. compare and represent fractions with denominators of 2,4,8; 3,6; 5,10 using an area model and a discrete model *(B; L4)*
9. subdivide a rectangle by length and width to show the multiplicative relationship between fractions *(B; L4)*
10. order common fractions with related denominators using diagrams and number lines *(B; L5)*
11. subtract a unit fraction from any whole number including 1 using the complement principle *(A; L6)*
12. use equivalence to add and subtract fractional quantities with related denominators *(B; L6, L7)*
13. solve word problems involving adding or subtracting fractional quantities with related denominators *(B; L7)*
14. assign probabilities to outcomes (not equally likely) using fractions (denominators 2,3,4,5,6,8,10) *(CHAN-B; L8)*
15. use knowledge of benchmark fractions, decimals and percentages to describe/assign the likelihood of outcomes *(CHAN-B; L8)*

### 🔴 RED — recall & single procedures (mostly RQF-A + benchmark recall)

**R1 — The whole and different-sized wholes**
*"I know the number 1 is the whole, and I can compare halves and quarters of different-sized wholes."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(paper strips / fraction tiles)*
Covers SC 1, 2. *Representing quantity fractions **A** (`MA3-RQF-02`). Content points: "Recognise the role of the number 1 as representing the whole"; "Compare halves and quarters of different-sized wholes; justify the need for a common whole."*

**R2 — Fraction, decimal and percentage equivalence**
*"I know % means per cent and 100% is the whole, and I can recall benchmarks like ½ = 50% = 0.5, ¼ = 25%, ¾ = 75%."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(hundred grid)*
Covers SC 4, 5. *Represents numbers **B** (`MA3-RN-03`). Content points: "Recognise that % means percent and 100% is the whole amount"; "Recall commonly used equivalent percentages, decimals and fractions."*

**R3 — Find fractions of whole numbers**
*"I can find a half, quarter, fifth or tenth of a whole number (e.g. ¼ of 20 = 5, 1/10 of 40 = 4)."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(tape diagram)*
Covers SC 6, 7. *Representing quantity fractions **B** / `MA3-RN-03`. Content points: "Find fractional quantities of whole numbers (halves, quarters, fifths and tenths)"; "Represent common percentages of quantities as fractions/decimals; find 10% of a quantity."*

**R4 — Subtract a unit fraction from a whole (complement)**
*"I can subtract a unit fraction from a whole number using the complement principle (e.g. 1 − ⅓ = ⅔; 3 − ¼ = 2¾)."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(fraction wall / number line)*
Covers SC 11. *Representing quantity fractions **A** (`MA3-RQF-02`). Content point: "Use diagrams, objects and mental strategies to subtract a unit fraction from any whole number including 1 (the complement principle)."*

## Stage 0 gate — decisions (made under full-autonomy run)

1. **Band assignment uses the genuine A/B labels** — Unit 36's DoE SC tables explicitly mark RQF **A**
   content (the whole, different-sized wholes, complement principle) and RQF **B** content (build whole,
   related denominators, equivalence, word problems). So **A→Red / B→Yellow** is used here (supported by
   both the labels and cognitive demand), unlike the cognitive-demand-only banding of Units 33–35.
2. **Overlap with Units 24/35 acknowledged** — benchmark FDP and finding 10% are revisited (also in
   Unit 35). Unit 36's distinctive content is *different-sized wholes, related denominators (area model),
   the complement principle, fraction word problems, and chance with fractions*.
3. **Green = Stage 4 group A**: Fractions `MA4-FRC-C-01` (different denominators, multiply) + Probability
   `MA4-PRO-C-01` (sample space, complementary events). Both verified in the NSW K-10 Syllabus (2022).
