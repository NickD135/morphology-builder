# Unit 33 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 Year B — Unit 33
**Topic:** Multiplicative relations — multiplication of large numbers, division, decimals × powers of 10,
benchmark-decimal multiplication, rates, number patterns, and brackets / order of operations
**Source:** `DoE Unit 33.docx`, 8 lessons (this DoE doc uses a "Daily number sense / Core concept"
LI-SC template and "Too hard? / Too easy?" differentiation — the core-concept SC are the unit content).
**Strands / outcomes:**
- `MA3-MR-01` — *selects and applies appropriate strategies to solve multiplication and division problems* (Lessons 1–8)
- `MA3-MR-02` — *constructs and completes number sentences involving multiplicative relations, applying the order of operations* (brackets / order of operations, inverse operations)
- `MA3-RN-01` — *applies place value… to represent the natural numbers to billions* (regrouping, daily number sense)
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **Note on overlap with Unit 31.** Both are Multiplicative-Relations Year-B units and share some content
> (decimals × powers of 10, the rule of a table, inverse operations). Unit 33's distinctive content is
> **4-digit multiplication, rates, benchmark-decimal multiplication, and brackets / order of operations as
> Stage 3 content**. (In Unit 31, order of operations appeared only as a *Stage 4* Green outcome using
> integers — `MA4-INT-C-01`. Here the DoE unit teaches order of operations / grouping symbols with whole
> numbers as **Stage 3 group B** core content — `MA3-MR-01/MR-02` — so it is **Yellow**, not Green.)

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·
*(sub-strand + content group + content point cited)*

**Band-rule note.** Like Unit 32, Unit 33 is almost entirely content group **B** (this is the MR-B home
unit); the only group-A content is *regroup numbers* (`MA3-RN-01` A, a daily warm-up). A strict
A→Red/B→Yellow split is unusable, so Unit 33 is banded by **SOLO cognitive demand** within the Stage 3
content (single-step recall/procedure → Red; applying / word problems / multi-step → Yellow), with the
content-group letter shown per outcome. Green = Stage 4 A.

### 🔴 RED — recall & single procedures

**R1 — Regroup numbers in different forms**
*"I can regroup a whole number into different forms (e.g. 3 450 000 = 3450 thousands)."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(place-value chart / number expander)*
Covers SC 1. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-01`). Whole numbers: Apply place value to partition, regroup and rename numbers to 1 billion — content point: "Regroup numbers in different forms (Reasons about quantity)."*

**R2 — Grouping symbols and the order of operations**
*"I know we must agree on the order to do operations, and I can use grouping symbols () to show which operation is done first."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 2, 3. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01` / `MA3-MR-02`). Explore the use of brackets and the order of operations to write number sentences — content points: "Recognise the need to agree on the order in which to perform operations"; "Use grouping symbols () in number sentences to indicate operations that must be performed first."*

**R3 — Multiply and divide decimals by powers of 10**
*"I can multiply and divide a decimal by 10, 100 and 1000 by comparing the relative place value of the digits."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(number slider / place-value chart)*
Covers SC 10. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). Multiply and divide decimals by powers of 10 — content point: "Compare the relative place value of digits to multiply and divide a decimal by powers of 10."*

**R4 — Why different division questions have the same answer**
*"I can explain why different division questions can have the same answer (e.g. 600 ÷ 20 = 60 ÷ 2)."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 7. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). Select and apply strategies to solve problems involving multiplication and division — content point: "Determine why different division questions have the same answer (Reasons about relations)."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying, multi-step & word problems

**Y1 — Multiply whole numbers up to 4 digits by 1- and 2-digit numbers**
*"I can select and use efficient strategies (area model, distributive property, algorithm) to multiply whole numbers up to 4 digits by 1- and 2-digit numbers."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(area-model grid)*
Covers SC 6. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). content point: "Select and use efficient strategies to multiply whole numbers of up to 4 digits by one- and 2-digit numbers."*

**Y2 — Multiply benchmark decimals by single-digit numbers (mentally)**
*"I can use mental strategies to multiply benchmark decimals (e.g. 0.5, 0.25) by single-digit numbers."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 9. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). content point: "Use mental strategies to multiply benchmark decimals by single-digit numbers."*

**Y3 — Solve rate word problems**
*"I can solve word problems involving rates using multiplication and division (e.g. bricks per hour, cost per item)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(vertical double number line)*

**Y4 — Determine the rule of a table**
*"I can determine a rule describing the relationship between the bottom number and the top number in a table."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 12. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). Represent and describe number patterns formed by multiples — content point: "Determine a rule describing the relationship between the bottom number and the top number in a table (Algebraic reasoning)."*

**Y5 — Solve problems using grouping symbols and order of operations**
*"I can investigate the order of operations in real-life contexts and solve multi-step problems that use grouping symbols."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 4, 5. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01` / `MA3-MR-02`). content points: "Investigate the order of operations using real-life contexts"; "Solve problems involving grouping symbols."*

**Y6 — Use inverse operations to find unknown quantities**

Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 group A (verified against NESA K-10)

No DoE Unit 33 lesson teaches these — expected for Green. Green centres on **Ratios & Rates**
(`MA4-RAT-C-01`), the natural Stage-4 home for the unit's rates work, plus the integer extension of
order of operations.

**G1 — Represent and solve real-life rate problems**
*"I can represent information as a simplified rate, convert between units for a rate, and solve real-life rate problems including best buys."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y3. *Stage 4 — **Ratios and rates** (`MA4-RAT-C-01`). Content points: "Represent given information as a simplified rate"; "Convert between units for rates"; "Solve a variety of real-life problems involving rates"; "Examine financial applications of rates, such as best buys."*

**G2 — Ratios and the unitary method**
*"I can use a ratio to compare quantities, simplify a ratio, and use the unitary method to divide a quantity in a given ratio."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends the unit's multiplicative reasoning. *Stage 4 — **Ratios and rates** (`MA4-RAT-C-01`). Content points: "Use ratios to compare 2 or more quantities measured in the same units"; "Simplify ratios"; "Apply the unitary method to solve ratio problems"; "Divide a quantity in a given ratio."*

**G3 — Order of operations with integers**
*"I can apply the order of operations, including grouping symbols, to expressions involving positive and negative integers."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends R2/Y5. *Stage 4 — **Computation with integers** (`MA4-INT-C-01`). Content points: "Apply the 4 operations to integers"; "Solve problems involving grouping symbols with integers"; "Apply the order of operations to evaluate expressions involving integers, with and without the use of digital tools."*
