## 🔴 Red

### R1 — Fraction, decimal and percentage equivalence
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-02` / `MA3-RN-02` — *Make connections between benchmark fractions, decimals and percentages*:
  > "Recognise that the symbol % means percent"
  > "Identify and use equivalent percentages, decimals and fractions, such as ½, 50% and 0.5 to solve problems"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1, L2, L3** (Daily number sense).

### R2 — Convert between metric units of mass
- **NESA:** `MA3-NSM-01` (Non-spatial measure **B** — Mass) — *Convert between common metric units of mass*:
  > "Convert between kilograms and grams and between kilograms and tonnes"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1, L2, L3** (Core concept).

### R3 — Decimal notation for measurements (to 3 decimal places)
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-02` / `MA3-RN-01` (Represents numbers **B** — decimals) — *Connect decimal representations to the metric system*:
  > "Recognise the equivalence of whole-number and decimal representations of measurements of mass and length"
  > "Interpret decimal notation for masses and lengths"
  > "Record mass and length using decimal notation of up to 3 decimal places"
- **DoE lesson:** **L3, L4** (Core concept).

### R4 — Build the whole from a fractional part
- **NESA:** `MA3-RQF-02` (Representing quantity fractions) — *Build up to the whole from a given fractional part*:
  > "Generate the whole quantity from non-unit fractional parts"
- **DoE lesson:** **L5, L6, L7** (Daily number sense).
