# Unit 34 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 Year B — Unit 34
**Topic:** *What needs to be measured determines the unit of measurement* — metric **mass** (g, kg, tonnes),
metric **length** (mm, cm, m, km), **perimeter** of rectangles (same-area comparisons), **decimal notation**
for measurements to 3 dp, **decimal addition/subtraction** word problems, plus daily **fraction–decimal–
percentage** equivalence and **building the whole from a fractional part**.
**Source:** `DoE Unit 34.docx`, 8 lessons (this DoE doc uses a "Daily number sense / Core concept" LI-SC
template with "Too hard? / Too easy?" differentiation — the core-concept SC are the unit content; two
daily-number-sense strands, FDP equivalence and build-the-whole, are taught as warm-ups across the unit).
**Strands / outcomes:**
- `MA3-NSM-01` — *selects and uses the appropriate unit and device to measure the masses of objects* (Lessons 1–3)
- `MA3-GM-02` — *selects and uses the appropriate unit and device to measure lengths and distances including perimeters* (Lessons 5–8)
- `MA3-RN-02` — *compares and orders decimals up to 3 decimal places* (decimal notation for measurements; Lessons 3–4)
- `MA3-RQF-02` — *determines ½, ¼, ⅕ and ⅓ of measures and quantities* (FDP equivalence; build-the-whole; daily)
- `MA3-RN-01` — *applies place value… to represent the properties of numbers* (decimal/whole-number equivalence)
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

> **Multi-strand unit.** Unlike the single-strand Units 29–33, Unit 34 deliberately weaves **mass, length,
> perimeter and decimals** around one big idea (the *unit* of measurement). The success criteria therefore
> span four Stage 3 sub-strands. Outcomes below are grouped by skill, not by strand, and each carries its
> sub-strand + content-group letter.

## 0a. Flat pool of extracted success criteria ("Students can …")

Extracted verbatim from each lesson's **Core concept** (and **Daily number sense**) LI/SC tables,
de-duplicated. Daily-number-sense strands are genuine unit content here (the build-the-whole and FDP
warm-ups are explicitly taught and assessed), so they are retained.

1. recognise that the symbol % means percent *(daily, L1–L3)*
2. identify and use equivalent percentages, decimals and fractions (e.g. ½, 50%, 0.5) to solve problems *(daily, L1–L3)*
3. generate the whole quantity from non-unit fractional parts *(daily, L5–L7)*
4. convert between kilograms and grams and between kilograms and tonnes *(L1, L2, L3)*
5. solve problems involving different units of mass *(L1, L2, L3)*
6. interpret decimal notation for masses (and lengths) *(L3, L4)*
7. recognise the equivalence of whole-number and decimal representations of measurements of mass and length *(L4)*
8. record mass and length using decimal notation of up to 3 decimal places *(L4)*
9. apply known strategies (levelling, addition for subtraction, constant difference, bridging) to add/subtract *(L3)*
10. solve word problems involving the addition and subtraction of decimals; justify the strategy used *(L6)*
11. investigate and compare perimeters of rectangles with the same area *(L5, L6)*
12. determine the number of different rectangles that can be formed using whole-number dimensions for a given area *(L5, L6)*
13. solve a variety of problems involving length and perimeter, including problems involving different units of length *(L5, L6, L7, L8)*
14. explain and use the relationship between the size of a unit and the number of units needed *(L7)*
15. use the decimal place value system to convert between metres and kilometres *(L8)*
16. convert measurements to the same unit to compare lengths and distances *(L8)*

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·
*(sub-strand + content group + content point cited)*

**Band-rule note.** Like Units 32 and 33, Unit 34's core content is almost entirely content group **B**
(the *new* measurement learning for Year 6 — the DoE unit's "Student prior learning" section confirms the
group-A measurement work, e.g. choosing units/devices and basic measuring, was the prior year). A strict
A→Red/B→Yellow split is therefore unusable, so Unit 34 is banded by **SOLO cognitive demand** within the
Stage 3 content (single-step recall/procedure → Red; applying / multi-step / word problems → Yellow), with
the content-group letter shown per outcome. Green = Stage 4 A.

### 🔴 RED — recall & single procedures

**R1 — Fraction, decimal and percentage equivalence**
*"I know % means 'per cent' (out of 100), and I can swap between benchmark fractions, decimals and percentages (e.g. ½ = 50% = 0.5, ¼ = 25% = 0.25)."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 1, 2. *Represents quantity fractions / FDP (`MA3-RQF-02`, `MA3-RN-02`). Make connections between benchmark fractions, decimals and percentages — content points: "Recognise that the symbol % means percent"; "Identify and use equivalent percentages, decimals and fractions to solve problems."*

**R2 — Convert between metric units of mass**
*"I can convert between grams and kilograms, and between kilograms and tonnes."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(kitchen/bathroom scales, metric weights)*
Covers SC 4. *Non-spatial measure **B** — Mass (`MA3-NSM-01`). Convert between common metric units of mass — content point: "Convert between kilograms and grams and between kilograms and tonnes."*

**R3 — Decimal notation for measurements (to 3 decimal places)**
*"I can read and record a mass or length using decimal notation up to 3 decimal places, and I know a whole-number and a decimal can name the same measurement (e.g. 2500 g = 2.5 kg)."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(place-value chart)*
Covers SC 6, 7, 8. *Represents numbers **B** — decimals (`MA3-RN-02` / `MA3-RN-01`). Content points: "Recognise the equivalence of whole-number and decimal representations of measurements of mass and length"; "Interpret decimal notation for masses and lengths"; "Record mass and length using decimal notation of up to 3 decimal places."*

**R4 — Build the whole from a fractional part**
*"I can work out the whole quantity when I am given a non-unit fractional part (e.g. if ¾ is 18, the whole is 24)."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(fraction bar / counters)*
Covers SC 3. *Representing quantity fractions (`MA3-RQF-02`). Build up to the whole — content point: "Generate the whole quantity from non-unit fractional parts (Reasons about relations)."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — applying, multi-step & word problems

**Y1 — Solve problems involving different units of mass**
*"I can solve word problems that mix units of mass (g, kg, tonnes), converting as needed."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 5. *Non-spatial measure **B** — Mass (`MA3-NSM-01`). content point: "Solve problems involving different units of mass."*

**Y2 — Convert and compare metric units of length**
*"I can use the decimal place-value system to convert between metres and kilometres, convert measurements to the same unit to compare lengths and distances, and I understand that a larger unit needs a smaller measure."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(conversion chart)*
Covers SC 14, 15, 16. *Geometric measure **B** — Length (`MA3-GM-02`). content points: "Use the decimal place value system to convert between metres and kilometres"; "Convert measurements to the same unit to compare lengths and distances"; "Explain and use the relationship between the size of a unit and the number of units needed."*

**Y3 — Compare perimeters of rectangles with the same area**
*"I can investigate rectangles that have the same area and compare their perimeters."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(1 cm grid paper)*
Covers SC 11. *Geometric measure **B** — Length/perimeter (`MA3-GM-02`). content point: "Investigate and compare perimeters of rectangles with the same area."*

**Y4 — Find the rectangles for a given area**
*"I can determine how many different rectangles with whole-number sides can be made for a given area."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(1 cm grid paper)*
Covers SC 12. *Geometric measure **B** — Length/perimeter (`MA3-GM-02`). content point: "Determine the number of different rectangles that can be formed using whole-number dimensions for a given area."*

**Y5 — Solve length and perimeter problems (mixed units)**
*"I can solve a variety of length and perimeter problems, including ones that mix units of length."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 13. *Geometric measure **B** — Length/perimeter (`MA3-GM-02`). content point: "Solve a variety of problems involving length and perimeter, including problems involving different units of length."*

**Y6 — Add and subtract decimals to solve word problems**
*"I can solve word problems that involve adding and subtracting decimals, and I can justify the strategy I chose."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 9, 10. *Additive relations **B** (`MA3-AR-01`). content points: "Solve word problems involving the addition and subtraction of decimals"; "Justify why the strategy used to solve addition and subtraction word problems is appropriate" (applying levelling, constant difference, bridging).*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 group A (verified against NESA K-10)

No DoE Unit 34 lesson teaches these — expected for Green. Green extends the unit's **measurement** theme
into Stage 4: from rectangle perimeter/area → **area of triangles & parallelograms and composite shapes**
(`MA4-ARE-C-01`), and from metric-length perimeter → **perimeter of composite shapes & circumference of
circles** (`MA4-LEN-C-01`).

**G1 — Area of triangles and parallelograms**
*"I can use a formula to find the area of a triangle (½ × base × height) and a parallelogram (base × height)."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y3/Y4. *Stage 4 — **Area and surface area** (`MA4-ARE-C-01`). Content points: "Establish and use the formula to find the area of a parallelogram"; "Establish and use the formula to find the area of a triangle."*

**G2 — Area of composite shapes and converting area units**
*"I can find the area of a composite (L-shape) figure by splitting it into rectangles, and I can convert between square units (cm² and m²)."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y4. *Stage 4 — **Area and surface area** (`MA4-ARE-C-01`). Content points: "Find the area of composite figures by dissection into known shapes"; "Convert between metric units of area (cm², m²)."*

**G3 — Perimeter of composite shapes and circumference of circles**
*"I can find the perimeter of a composite shape, and I can calculate the circumference of a circle using C = π × d."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y3/Y5. *Stage 4 — **Length** (`MA4-LEN-C-01`). Content points: "Find the perimeter of a range of plane shapes including composite figures"; "Investigate the circumference of a circle and calculate it using C = πd."*
