# Unit 31 — Stage 0 Draft Rubric

**Unit:** Mathematics Stage 3 Year B — Unit 31
**Topic:** Number — place value to 1 billion & decimals to thousandths; percentages; multiplicative
patterns; multiplication & division; multiply/divide decimals by powers of 10; missing numbers
**Source:** `DoE Unit 31.zip` → `Mathematics-Stage3-Unit31.docx`, 8 lessons
**Strands / outcomes:**
- `MA3-RN-01` — *applies an understanding of place value and the role of zero to represent the natural numbers to billions* (Lesson 1)
- `MA3-RN-02` — *compares and orders decimals up to 3 decimal places* (Lesson 2)
- `MA3-RN-03` — *determines percentages of quantities, and finds equivalent fractions and decimals for benchmark percentage values* (Lesson 3)
- `MA3-MR-01` — *selects and applies appropriate strategies to solve multiplication and division problems* (Lessons 4–7)
- `MA3-MR-02` — *constructs and completes number sentences involving multiplicative relations, applying the order of operations to calculations* (Lesson 8)
- `MAO-WM-01` — Working mathematically (throughout)

## 0a. Flat pool of extracted success criteria ("Students can …")

Extracted verbatim from each lesson's LI/SC **table**, de-duplicated; lesson(s) noted (cross-checked
against the Outcomes-and-content × lesson grid, Table 23).

**Whole numbers & decimals (Lessons 1–2, RN-01 / RN-02 — group A):**
1. recognise 1000 thousands is 1 million and 1000 millions is 1 billion *(L1)*
2. regroup numbers in different forms *(L1)*
3. partition numbers to 1 billion in non-standard forms *(L1)*
4. express thousandths as decimals *(L2)*
5. interpret decimal notation for thousandths *(L2)*
6. indicate the place value of digits in decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places *(L2)*
7. interpret zero digit(s) at the end of a decimal *(L2)*

**Percentages (Lesson 3, RN-03 — group B):**
8. recognise that the symbol % means percent and 100% is the whole amount *(L3)*
9. represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals *(L3)*
10. recognise that 10% is one-tenth of 100% and use this to find 10% of a quantity *(L3)*

**Multiplicative patterns (Lesson 4, MR-01 — group B):**
11. use a given geometric pattern involving multiples to create a table of values *(L4)*
12. describe a pattern formed by multiples in words, in terms of multiplication rather than addition *(L4)*
13. determine a rule describing the relationship between the bottom number and the top number in a table *(L4)*

**Multiplication & division (Lessons 5–6, MR-01 — group A):**
14. factorise numbers to aid mental multiplication *(L5)*
15. extend the area model to represent 2-digit by 2-digit multiplication *(L5)*
16. use a multiplication algorithm with understanding *(L5)*
17. solve multiplication word problems *(L5)*
18. apply and record appropriate strategies to solve division word problems *(L6)*
19. use and interpret remainders in solutions to division problems *(L6)*
20. use digital technologies to divide whole numbers by one- and 2-digit divisors *(L6)*

**Decimals × powers of 10 & missing numbers (Lessons 7–8, MR-01 B / MR-02 B):**
21. compare the relative place value of digits to multiply and divide a decimal by powers of 10 *(L7)*
22. complete number sentences that involve more than one operation by calculating missing numbers *(L8)*
23. identify and use inverse operations to assist with the solution of number sentences *(L8)*

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·
*(sub-strand + content group + content point cited)*

**Band rule — Unit 31 fits the validated A→Red / B→Yellow rule cleanly.** This unit pairs the A and B
content groups across the Number strands: Represents Numbers A (place value, decimals) + B
(percentages); Multiplicative Relations A (multiply/divide) + B (patterns, decimals × powers of 10,
number sentences). The DoE unit treats the **A** content (place value, decimals, the multiplication &
division algorithms) as the consolidation/fluency foundation and the **B** content (percentages,
pattern rules, place-value shifts, equivalent number sentences) as the new applying work — so
**RN-A + MR-A → Red**, **RN-B + MR-B → Yellow**, **Green → Stage 4 A**.

### 🔴 RED — Stage 3 group A (place value, decimals & the multiply/divide algorithms)

**R1 — Partition, regroup and rename whole numbers to 1 billion**
*"I can partition, regroup and rename numbers up to 1 billion in standard and non-standard forms, knowing 1000 thousands = 1 million and 1000 millions = 1 billion."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(place-value chart / MAB / number expander)*
Covers SC 1–3. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-01`). Whole numbers: Apply place value to partition, regroup and rename numbers to 1 billion — content points: "Recognise 1000 thousands is 1 million and 1000 millions is 1 billion"; "Regroup numbers in different forms (Reasons about quantity)"; "Partition numbers to 1 billion in non-standard forms."*

**R2 — Express and interpret decimals to thousandths**
*"I can express thousandths as decimals, interpret decimals to 3 places, name the place value of each digit, and interpret zeros at the end of a decimal."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(place-value houses optional)*
Covers SC 4–7. *Represents numbers **A** (`MA3-RN-02`). Decimals and percentages — content points: "Express thousandths as decimals"; "Interpret decimal notation for thousandths"; "Indicate the place value of digits in decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places"; "Interpret zero digit(s) at the end of a decimal."*

**R3 — Multiply 2- and 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers**
*"I can multiply 2- and 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers using factorising, the area model and the multiplication algorithm, and solve multiplication word problems."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(area-model grid paper)*
Covers SC 14–17. *Multiplicative relations **A** (`MA3-MR-01`). Select and apply strategies to multiply 2- and 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers — content points: "Factorise numbers to aid mental multiplication"; "Extend the area model to represent 2-digit by 2-digit multiplication"; "Use a multiplication algorithm with understanding (Reasons about relations)"; "Solve multiplication word problems."*

**R4 — Divide a 3+ digit number by a one-digit divisor, interpreting remainders**
*"I can divide a number with 3 or more digits by a one-digit divisor, solving division word problems and interpreting the remainder in context."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 18–20. *Multiplicative relations **A** (`MA3-MR-01`). Select and apply strategies to divide a number with 3 or more digits by a one-digit divisor — content points: "Apply and record appropriate strategies to solve division word problems"; "Use and interpret remainders in solutions to division problems"; "Use digital technologies to divide whole numbers by one- and 2-digit divisors."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — Stage 3 group B (percentages, pattern rules, place-value shifts, number sentences)

**Y1 — Percentages: % and 100% as the whole; common percentages as fractions and decimals**
*"I know % means percent and 100% is the whole, and I can represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(percentage bar / 100-grid)*
Covers SC 8, 9. *Represents numbers **B** (`MA3-RN-03`). Decimals and percentages: Make connections between benchmark fractions, decimals and percentages — content points: "Recognise that the symbol % means percent and 100% is the whole amount"; "Represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals."*

**Y2 — Find 10% of a quantity**
*"I can use the fact that 10% is one-tenth of 100% to find 10% of a quantity."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 10. *Represents numbers **B** (`MA3-RN-03`). content point: "Recognise that 10% is one-tenth of 100% and use this to find 10% of a quantity (Reasons about relations)."*

**Y3 — Number patterns formed by multiples: build a table and describe as multiplication**
*"I can use a geometric pattern involving multiples to build a table of values, and describe the pattern in words as multiplication rather than addition."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(pattern tiles / counters)*
Covers SC 11, 12. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). Represent and describe number patterns formed by multiples — content points: "Use a given geometric pattern involving multiples to create a table of values"; "Describe a pattern formed by multiples in words, in terms of multiplication rather than addition."*

**Y4 — Determine the rule relating the two rows of a table**
*"I can determine a rule describing the relationship between the bottom number and the top number in a table (algebraic reasoning)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 13. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). content point: "Determine a rule describing the relationship between the bottom number and the top number in a table (Algebraic reasoning)."*

**Y5 — Multiply and divide decimals by powers of 10**
*"I can multiply and divide a decimal by powers of 10 (10, 100, 1000) by comparing the relative place value of the digits."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(place-value slider)*
Covers SC 21. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-01`). Multiply and divide decimals by powers of 10 — content point: "Compare the relative place value of digits to multiply and divide a decimal by powers of 10."*

**Y6 — Equivalent number sentences: find missing numbers using inverse operations**
*"I can complete number sentences with more than one operation by calculating missing numbers, using inverse operations to help."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Covers SC 22, 23. *Multiplicative relations **B** (`MA3-MR-02`). Use equivalent number sentences involving multiplication and division to find unknown quantities — content points: "Complete number sentences that involve more than one operation by calculating missing numbers"; "Identify and use inverse operations to assist with the solution of number sentences."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 group A (Number — verified against NESA K-10)
