## 🔴 Red

### R1 — Partition, regroup and rename whole numbers to 1 billion
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-01` (Represents numbers **A**) — *Whole numbers: Apply place value to partition, regroup and rename numbers to 1 billion*:
  > "Recognise 1000 thousands is 1 million and 1000 millions is 1 billion"
  > "Regroup numbers in different forms (Reasons about quantity)"
  > "Partition numbers to 1 billion in non-standard forms"
- **DoE lesson:** **L1** (Regrouping and partitioning numbers; Table 23 column 1).

### R2 — Express and interpret decimals to thousandths
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-02` (Represents numbers **A**) — *Decimals and percentages: Recognise that the place value system can be extended beyond hundredths* and *Compare, order and represent decimals*:
  > "Express thousandths as decimals"
  > "Interpret decimal notation for thousandths"
  > "Indicate the place value of digits in decimal numbers of up to 3 decimal places"
  > "Interpret zero digit(s) at the end of a decimal"
- **DoE lesson:** **L2** (Representing thousandths; Table 23 column 2).

### R3 — Multiply 2- and 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers
- **NESA:** `MA3-MR-01` (Multiplicative relations **A**) — *Select and apply mental and written strategies to multiply 2- and 3-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers*:
  > "Factorise numbers to aid mental multiplication"
  > "Extend the area model to represent 2-digit by 2-digit multiplication"
  > "Use a multiplication algorithm with understanding (Reasons about relations)"
  > "Solve multiplication word problems"
- **DoE lesson:** **L5** (Multiplication word problems; Table 23 column 5).

### R4 — Divide a 3+ digit number by a one-digit divisor, interpreting remainders
- **NESA:** `MA3-MR-01` (Multiplicative relations **A**) — *Select and apply strategies to divide a number with 3 or more digits by a one-digit divisor*:
  > "Apply and record appropriate strategies to solve division word problems"
  > "Use and interpret remainders in solutions to division problems"
  > "Use digital technologies to divide whole numbers by one- and 2-digit divisors"
- **DoE lesson:** **L6** (Division word problems; Table 23 column 6).

## 🟡 Yellow

### Y1 — Percentages: % and 100% as the whole; common percentages as fractions and decimals
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-03` (Represents numbers **B**) — *Decimals and percentages: Make connections between benchmark fractions, decimals and percentages*:
  > "Recognise that the symbol % means percent and 100% is the whole amount"
  > "Represent common percentages of quantities and lengths as fractions and decimals"
- **DoE lesson:** **L3** (Representing percentages; Table 23 column 3).

### Y2 — Find 10% of a quantity
- **NESA:** `MA3-RN-03` (Represents numbers **B**):
  > "Recognise that 10% is one-tenth of 100% and use this to find 10% of a quantity (Reasons about relations)"
- **DoE lesson:** **L3** (Representing percentages; Table 23 column 3).

### Y3 — Number patterns formed by multiples: build a table and describe as multiplication
- **NESA:** `MA3-MR-01` (Multiplicative relations **B**) — *Represent and describe number patterns formed by multiples*:
  > "Use a given geometric pattern involving multiples to create a table of values"
  > "Describe a pattern formed by multiples in words, in terms of multiplication rather than addition"
- **DoE lesson:** **L4** (Multiplicative patterns; Table 23 column 4).

### Y4 — Determine the rule relating the two rows of a table
- **NESA:** `MA3-MR-01` (Multiplicative relations **B**):
  > "Determine a rule describing the relationship between the bottom number and the top number in a table (Algebraic reasoning)"
- **DoE lesson:** **L4** (Multiplicative patterns; Table 23 column 4).

### Y5 — Multiply and divide decimals by powers of 10
- **NESA:** `MA3-MR-01` (Multiplicative relations **B**) — *Multiply and divide decimals by powers of 10*:
  > "Compare the relative place value of digits to multiply and divide a decimal by powers of 10"

### Y6 — Equivalent number sentences: find missing numbers using inverse operations
- **NESA:** `MA3-MR-02` (Multiplicative relations **B**) — *Use equivalent number sentences involving multiplication and division to find unknown quantities*:

- **DoE lesson:** **L8** (Calculating missing numbers; Table 23 column 8).
