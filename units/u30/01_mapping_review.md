- **NESA:** `MA3-GM-03` (GM **A**) — *Angles: Estimate, measure and compare angles using degrees*:

### R3 — Create angles with a protractor; classify acute, obtuse, reflex

  > "Identify and describe angle size in degrees for the classifications acute, obtuse and reflex"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L4** (create angles) and **L5** (classify acute/obtuse/reflex; Table 26 ticks columns 4 & 5).

### R4 — Compare 12- and 24-hour time, convert, and read timetables

- **DoE lesson(s):** **L6** (24-hour time introduced) and **L8** (convert + timetables; Table 26 ticks columns 6 & 8).

- **NESA:** `MA3-GM-03` (GM **B**) — *Angles: Investigate the relationships formed by the intersection of straight lines*:
  > "Recognise that perpendicular lines intersect at right angles (90°)"
  > "Identify angle types formed by the intersection of straight lines, including right angles (90°), angles on a straight line (add to 180°) and angles at a point that form an angle of revolution (add to 360°)"

  > "Investigate adjacent angles on a straight line and establish that they add to 180°"
- **DoE lesson(s):** **L2** (Adjacent angles that form a right or straight angle) and **L5** (Table 26 columns 2 & 5).

### Y4 — Angles at a point add to 360° (angle of revolution)

All map to **Stage 4 Angle relationships `MA4-ANG-C-01`** (syllabus p.62). No DoE Unit 30 lesson — these
are original extension mini-lessons.

### G1 — Complementary, supplementary and vertically opposite angles
- **NESA:** `MA4-ANG-C-01` — *Identify geometrical properties of angles at a point*:
  > "Identify right angles, straight angles, angles of complete revolution and vertically opposite angles"
  > "Apply the terms complementary and supplementary to a pair of angles adding to 90° and 180°, respectively"
  > "Apply the term adjacent angles to a pair of angles with a common arm and common vertex"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original). Extends Y3.

### G2 — Find unknown angles using angle relationships and reasoning
- **NESA:** `MA4-ANG-C-01` — *Solve numerical problems involving angles using reasoning*:
  > "Apply the knowledge of angle relationships including angles at a point to find the sizes of unknown angles embedded in diagrams and give reasons"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original). Extends Y1/Y2/Y4.

### G3 — Angles formed by a transversal across parallel lines
- **NESA:** `MA4-ANG-C-01` — *Identify and describe corresponding, alternate and co-interior angles when 2 straight lines are crossed by a transversal, including parallel lines*:
  > "Identify, name and measure alternate angle pairs, corresponding angle pairs and co-interior angle pairs for 2 lines cut by a transversal"
  > "Verify and identify corresponding angles and alternate angles as equal, and co-interior angles as supplementary, when a pair of parallel lines is cut by a transversal"
  > "Apply the knowledge of angles associated with parallel lines to find the sizes of unknown angles embedded in diagrams"
- **DoE lesson:** none (original). Extends Y1.
