**Angles (Lessons 1–5, GM-03):**
1. measure angles of up to 360° using a protractor *(L3)*
2. estimate and describe the size of angles using known angles as benchmarks *(L4)*
3. record angle measurements using the symbol for degrees (°) *(L4)*
4. create angles of up to 360° using a protractor *(L4, L5)*
5. identify and describe angle size in degrees for the classifications acute, obtuse and reflex *(L5)*
6. recognise that perpendicular lines intersect at right angles (90°) *(L1)*
7. identify angle types formed by the intersection of straight lines, including right angles (90°), angles on a straight line (add to 180°) and angles at a point that form an angle of revolution (add to 360°) *(L1)*
8. recognise right angles, angles on a straight line and angles at a point embedded in diagrams *(L3, L4)*
9. investigate adjacent angles that form a right angle and establish that they add to 90° *(L2, L5)*
10. investigate adjacent angles on a straight line and establish that they add to 180° *(L2, L5)*
11. investigate angles at a point and establish that they form an angle of revolution and add to 360° *(L3, L5)*

**Time (Lessons 6–8, NSM-02):**
12. recognise that 24-hour time is used to avoid confusion between am and pm *(L6)*
13. convert between 24-hour time and 12-hour time using am or pm notation *(L8)*
14. read, interpret and use timetables from real-life situations, involving 12- and 24-hour time *(L8)*
15. use start and finish times to calculate the elapsed time of events *(L6, L7, L8)*
16. add and subtract time mentally using bridging strategies *(L6, L7)*
17. represent commonly used time intervals as decimals *(L7)*
18. solve a variety of problems involving duration, including where times are expressed in 12-hour and 24-hour notation *(L7, L8)*

## 0b–0c. Classified, banded rubric

Format per outcome: **Code — Title** · descriptor · band · **[modality tag]** ·
*(sub-strand + content group + content point cited)*

**Band rule — Unit 30 fits the validated A→Red / B→Yellow rule cleanly** (unlike Unit 29). This unit
deliberately pairs the **A and B** content groups of each strand: Geometric Measure A (measure /
create / classify angles) **+** B (angle relationships), and Non-spatial Measure A (12/24-hour convert
& timetables) **+** B (elapsed time & duration). So **GM-A + NSM-A → Red**, **GM-B + NSM-B → Yellow**,
**Green → Stage 4 A** — confirmed against the DoE unit's own framing (the A content is the protractor /
clock fluency that the B relationship/duration work builds on).

### 🔴 RED — Stage 3 group A (measure & convert fluency)

**R1 — Measure angles up to 360° using a protractor**
*"I can use a protractor correctly to measure angles up to 360°, lining up the centre and base line and reading the right scale."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-required]** *(protractors; angle-identifier card)*
Covers SC 1. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-03`). Angles: Estimate, measure and compare angles using degrees — content point: "Measure angles of up to 360° using a protractor."*

**R2 — Estimate angles using benchmarks and record using the degrees symbol**
*"I can estimate an angle's size using benchmark angles (90°, 180°, 360°) and record measurements using the degrees symbol (°)."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(benchmark angle fans / rotations)*
Covers SC 2, 3. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-03`). content points: "Estimate and describe the size of angles using known angles as benchmarks (Reasons about mental rotation)"; "Record angle measurements using the symbol for degrees (°)."*

**R3 — Create angles with a protractor and classify them as acute, obtuse or reflex**
*"I can construct an angle of a given size with a protractor, and classify angles as acute, obtuse or reflex."*
Band: **Red** · **[hands-on-required]** *(protractors, ruler)*
Covers SC 4, 5. *Geometric measure **A** (`MA3-GM-03`). Angles: Use a protractor to measure and identify types of angles — content points: "Create angles of up to 360° using a protractor"; "Identify and describe angle size in degrees for the classifications acute, obtuse and reflex."*

**R4 — Compare 12- and 24-hour time, convert between them, and read timetables**
*"I can explain why 24-hour time avoids am/pm confusion, convert between 24-hour and 12-hour time, and read and use 12- and 24-hour timetables."*
Band: **Red** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(timetables; Google Maps for travel times)*
Covers SC 12, 13, 14. *Non-spatial measure **A** (`MA3-NSM-02`). Time: Compare 12- and 24-hour time systems and convert between them — content points: "Recognise that 24-hour time is used to avoid confusion between am and pm"; "Convert between 24-hour time and 12-hour time using am or pm notation"; "Read, interpret and use timetables from real-life situations, involving 12- and 24-hour time."*

### 🟡 YELLOW — Stage 3 group B (angle relationships & duration)

**Y1 — Perpendicular lines and angle types formed by intersecting lines**
*"I can recognise that perpendicular lines meet at right angles (90°), and identify the angle types formed where straight lines intersect — right angles, angles on a straight line (180°) and angles at a point (360°)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(craft sticks; right-angle tester / set square)*
Covers SC 6, 7. *Geometric measure **B** (`MA3-GM-03`). Angles: Investigate the relationships formed by the intersection of straight lines — content points: "Recognise that perpendicular lines intersect at right angles (90°)"; "Identify angle types formed by the intersection of straight lines, including right angles (90°), angles on a straight line (add to 180°) and angles at a point that form an angle of revolution (add to 360°)."*

**Y2 — Recognise angles embedded in diagrams**
*"I can find and name right angles, angles on a straight line and angles at a point that are embedded inside diagrams and pictures."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(right-angle tester; images / building plans)*
Covers SC 8. *Geometric measure **B** (`MA3-GM-03`). Angles: Investigate angles on a straight line and angles at a point — content point: "Recognise right angles, angles on a straight line and angles at a point embedded in diagrams (Reasons about spatial orientation)."*

**Y3 — Adjacent angles that add to 90° and 180°**
*"I can investigate adjacent angles that form a right angle (add to 90°) and adjacent angles on a straight line (add to 180°), and find a missing adjacent angle."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(protractor; building/house plan)*
Covers SC 9, 10. *Geometric measure **B** (`MA3-GM-03`). content points: "Investigate adjacent angles that form a right angle and establish that they add to 90°"; "Investigate adjacent angles on a straight line and establish that they add to 180°."*

**Y4 — Angles at a point add to 360° (angle of revolution)**
*"I can investigate angles at a point and establish that they form an angle of revolution adding to 360°, and find a missing angle at a point."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-required]** *(protractor; pattern blocks)*
Covers SC 11. *Geometric measure **B** (`MA3-GM-03`). content point: "Investigate angles at a point and establish that they form an angle of revolution and add to 360°."*

**Y5 — Calculate elapsed time using bridging strategies**
*"I can use start and finish times to calculate elapsed time, adding and subtracting time mentally using bridging strategies (e.g. bridge to the next hour)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(manipulable geared clock)*
Covers SC 15, 16. *Non-spatial measure **B** (`MA3-NSM-02`). Time: Solve problems involving duration, using 12- and 24-hour time — content points: "Use start and finish times to calculate the elapsed time of events"; "Add and subtract time mentally using bridging strategies."*

**Y6 — Solve duration problems in 12- and 24-hour time; represent time intervals as decimals**
*"I can solve a variety of duration problems in 12- and 24-hour notation, and represent commonly used time intervals as decimals (e.g. 15 min = 0.25 h)."*
Band: **Yellow** · **[written/digital-appropriate]** *(timetables; geared clock support)*
Covers SC 17, 18. *Non-spatial measure **B** (`MA3-NSM-02`). content points: "Represent commonly used time intervals as decimals"; "Solve a variety of problems involving duration, including where times are expressed in 12-hour and 24-hour notation."*

### 🟢 GREEN — Stage 4 group A (Angle relationships — verified against NESA K-10)

No DoE Unit 30 lesson teaches these — expected for Green.

All three Green outcomes use **Stage 4 group A** content from the **Angle relationships** focus area
(`MA4-ANG-C-01` — *applies angle relationships to solve problems, including those related to
transversals on sets of parallel lines*). This is the genuine Stage-4 generalisation of the Yellow
angle-relationship work.

> ⚠️ Green extends the **Angles** strand only. NSW Stage 4 has **no Time focus area** — duration/time is
> a Stage 3 terminal topic — so there is no Stage 4 A content point to cite for a "time" Green outcome.
> Inventing one would breach spec §0d (must quote a real Stage 4 content point), so Green is angle-only.

**G1 — Complementary, supplementary and vertically opposite angles**
*"I can identify vertically opposite angles, and apply the terms complementary (adding to 90°) and supplementary (adding to 180°) to pairs of angles."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y3. *Stage 4 — **Angle relationships** (`MA4-ANG-C-01`). Content points: "Identify right angles, straight angles, angles of complete revolution and vertically opposite angles"; "Apply the terms complementary and supplementary to a pair of angles adding to 90° and 180°, respectively"; "Apply the term adjacent angles to a pair of angles with a common arm and common vertex."*

**G2 — Find unknown angles using angle relationships and reasoning**
*"I can use angle relationships at a point, on a line, and vertically opposite angles to find the size of unknown angles embedded in diagrams, giving reasons (including using a simple equation)."*
Band: **Green** · **[written/digital-appropriate]**
Extends Y1/Y2/Y4. *Stage 4 — **Angle relationships** (`MA4-ANG-C-01`). Content point: "Apply the knowledge of angle relationships including angles at a point to find the sizes of unknown angles embedded in diagrams and give reasons."*

**G3 — Angles formed by a transversal across parallel lines**
*"I can identify and name corresponding, alternate and co-interior angle pairs when two lines are crossed by a transversal, and use the fact that (for parallel lines) corresponding and alternate angles are equal and co-interior angles are supplementary to find unknown angles."*
Band: **Green** · **[hands-on-preferred]** *(parallel lines + transversal on grid paper)*
Extends Y1. *Stage 4 — **Angle relationships** (`MA4-ANG-C-01`). Content points: "Identify, name and measure alternate angle pairs, corresponding angle pairs and co-interior angle pairs for 2 lines cut by a transversal"; "Verify and identify corresponding angles and alternate angles as equal, and co-interior angles as supplementary, when a pair of parallel lines is cut by a transversal"; "Apply the knowledge of angles associated with parallel lines to find the sizes of unknown angles embedded in diagrams."*
